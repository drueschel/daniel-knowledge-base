## Inhalt:

let's explore the concept of containment in healing. It's a very interesting concept as you can see
 almost everything in the body as a container. The skin is a container for your body, a cell is a
 container, an organ is a container. Everything in a body is a container and contains something else.
 And just like that is in the physical sense, the body is a container. It's also in the emotional
 energetic sense functions as a container. So in emotional work, the container for emotions is
 built by our awareness, our body and our nervous system. So imagine a container like a house for
 your emotions, a house where your emotions live in. And just like a house that you want to live in,
 your emotions don't want to live in a jail cell with lots of boundary that are rigid. In that
 environment, living in a rigid body with rigid muscles and tight contracted tissue, in that
 environment, emotions cannot vibrate out and be felt, witnessed. But you also don't want an environment
 where there are no boundaries at all and the emotions can just fly everywhere around.
 If that's the case and if your energetic system is so open, you are most likely easily overwhelmed
 when you feel emotions. And from this understanding, generally have two types of people. We call them
 slow feelers and fast feelers, which is a spectrum of how well a person can contain and host their
 own emotions. So an emotion wants to live in a container that has enough space, but also provides
 enough boundaries to keep the emotion held and contained within the boundaries. So you don't
 want it to fly away, but you also don't want it to tight. So when you are a slow feeler,
 the problem is that you cannot allow emotions to be felt and processed because
 there are, in a sense, you're numb to your own emotions. In your container is not enough space
 for the emotion to show themselves and vibrate out. When you're a fast feeler, you don't have enough
 boundaries in your emotions, enough containment, and so you are easily overwhelmed from emotions. In
 that case, when we say you need to heal and feel your emotions, many of the fast feelers will say,
 "Well, I am feeling my emotions. All I do is feel emotions all day. I'm always overwhelmed."
 And that's exactly the problem. They're always overwhelmed by the emotion. They're unable to
 hold the emotion, to create that container for the emotion. And because they're unable to create
 that container, they're unable to go into the deeper emotions that are actually preventing
 the transformation from happening. What they're feeling is a cover emotion that is roaming around.
