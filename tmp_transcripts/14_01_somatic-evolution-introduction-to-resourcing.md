## Inhalt:

In our own awareness, all qualities always exist. Your vulnerability, your strength,
 your sadness, your joy, your safety, all these qualities are always present.
 It is just that our mind tends to block out certain qualities and this is due to trauma.
 By focusing our mind on certain aspects that we are, for example that we are sexual beings
 or that we are vulnerable beings or there are super powerful beings, we can reclaim that quality
 and awaken that aspect that was always there but feel a much deeper connection to it.
 Resourcing is a way in which we focus on the aspect of safety, comfort and pleasantness in the body
 and by bringing that aspect up, this is an aspect that has been lost by trauma,
 lost by the shutdown reaction and the fear and the fright of an event that caused overwhelm.
 We essentially establish safety in the nervous system and rewire the brain to feel safer
 in the body. By doing this, we can then introduce uncomfortable emotions and sensations back into
 the system but with the experience safety that is now also present and that fundamentally rewires
 the nervous system because it teaches the nervous system that that which seemed unwelcome,
 which seemed impossible, this is not allowed to exist, this emotion, this shame is not allowed to be
 there, is now allowed to exist because safety and the uncomfortable sensation can both be there at
 the same time and this is why resourcing is so important because when we come from a state of
 safety, we realize that we don't need to have a masochistic approach about healing ourselves,
 we don't need to go seek painful experience and thrust ourselves into the next shattering process
 but we can come from a gentleness and allow ourselves to meet and witness
 all that is uncomfortable that wants to come up in the moment but from a place of safety and gentleness
 and this is really the respect that we should have for ourselves for our nervous system.
 If we allow that other people treat us badly or if we treat ourselves harshly,
 then the only reason we do that is because of shame and guilt. Shame and guilt tend to
 estrange us from our natural sense of dignity and respect because when we feel worthless and
 shame, we try to become something more worthy but that many times ignores our own rhythm of healing
 in which we can stay within the bounds of our own safety and this is why resourcing
 is so important in the beginning of a facilitation or throughout because we can always come back to
 safety and then introduce the uncomfortable emotions back. This technique is often called
 "pendulation" by which we go back and forth between the uncomfortable emotion, the safety,
 the uncomfortable emotion and back to the safety again.
