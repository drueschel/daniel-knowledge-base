## Inhalt:

sadness is another inward destabilizing emotion. In order to feel sadness, to feel grief, we
 have to be in touch with ourselves. Sadness plays a big role when our soul feels like
 it's losing a part of itself. It can be an aspect of reality or it can be another person.
 The movement between that losing that connection, that's what sadness feels like. And when you
 ever watched a movie that moved you, it really moved sadness from you. That's the movement
 when it's coming together and reintegrating. But there's also the sadness where it's falling
 apart. So the friction between two parts of yourself that you identify with, that you
 want connection with, that movement feels as being experienced as sadness.
