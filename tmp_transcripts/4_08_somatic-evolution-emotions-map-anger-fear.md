## Inhalt:

So there are many different kinds of emotions. In our emotions map we break them down into inward
 destabilizing emotions and outward stabilizing emotions. Let's start with anger for example.
 Anger is a fiery strong outward stabilizing emotion. It draws our attention outwards
 away from our vulnerability so we can protect our territory, so we can fight for our survival,
 so we can protect the people we love. And this emotion is particularly useful for setting boundaries.
 Many people who struggle with anger and have a lot of anger repressed are people who are usually
 hyper empathic, who have a bias towards being nice and kind and who have poor boundaries.
 Those are the types of people who really struggle with repressed anger because there's no space
 created for that anger to actually be there and be witnessed. Then there's people who are
 cholerics for example, people who are always angry. Those people, they don't have an anger issue,
 they have a vulnerability issue. They're looping around in their anger because they're unable to
 come in touch with the most emotions which are more inward driven such as sadness and fear. Fear for
 example is on the opposite side of anger because it stabilizes us more inward. In a sense, it
 destabilizes our sense of self and we feel fragile, vulnerable, right? We feel like there is this,
 we're more in touch, we're all of a sudden, we're more in touch with the threats on the outside.
 We feel threatened. So fear and anger, fight and flight are the different reactions that can happen
 in a survival situation and that's why those are the basic flavors, core flavors of emotions.
