## Inhalt:

I just want to share with you some fundamental principles of healing. Number one, when working
 with emotions, it doesn't matter much which emotion is coming up, but what matters most is the
 relationship we have to the emotion. If a person doesn't know anger or if a person doesn't allow
 sadness, it is our job to create safety and give permission to express what couldn't be expressed
 and change the relationship that the client has to that emotion.
 Main principle number two, re-experiencing with safety and support. Because the experience that
 caused the imprint is always connected with a sense of loss of power, a sense of loss of safety
 and support re-experiencing these elements now with the support and safety that was missing
 completely rewires our brain and our neural pathways that can now shine in completely new
 perspective on the situation that was deemed unbearable. Number three, it's not your job
 to solve the person. You are only here to facilitate this process. Whatever wants to happen
 will happen and nothing more, nothing less. Number four, the body desperately wants to heal.
 We just have to move out of the way what doesn't. Number five, healing means witnessing.
 The healing in any case happens because we can witness what was unable to be witnessed before
 and we can only heal what we can feel and witness. Number six, welcoming the unwelcome.
 Allowing feelings thoughts and emotions to come up that were not welcome before is one of the
 primary healing mechanisms. It changes the fundamental relationship we have to ourselves
 and emotions. Seven, as long as you're present with your heart you can't do anything wrong.
 You're on a conscious exploration without goal and you're surrendering to the intelligence,
 the same intelligence that is in trees when they grow, the same intelligence that is building
 a child inside the womb of a mother, surrendering to the same intelligence in a emotional process.
 And you can only do that when you're present within your heart. Number eight,
 awareness itself is curative. Just being present with the awareness changes the way our ego reacts.
 This is the principle of mindfulness and meditation. Just being aware changes everything.
 Number nine, breaking the pattern. Do the opposite. So we have reacting to situation in the same way
 over and over again leaves us stuck in loops. But as facilitators we can interrupt the same
 reaction pattern. And through that we unlock previously hidden resources that give the client
 the opportunity to break out of this old pattern.
