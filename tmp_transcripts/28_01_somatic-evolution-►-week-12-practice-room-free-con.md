## Inhalt:

For week 12, we're taking a more creative route whereas phantom dialogue and parts work have kind of given you a clear structure, a clear path to to take your client or your practice partner down, meaning let's convert this to a person, or let's convert this to a part.
 In this week, we're going to look for the conversions that are already present and happening in a person's inner world.
 Because we can't really talk about anything going on in our inner world without symbols, without creating symbols.
 And the more we can learn to listen for them, the more we can help people to explore that inner world the way it already is without converting it into something different.
 So we're helping them to find out what the conversion is that is already happening and we call this the free conversion.
 As always, all the steps you already know and then really pay attention to if there already is a symbol, any form of conversion.
 And this may not look very obvious, but as people talk about a stone on their heart, cold showers going down their back, the heaviness of loss that is weighing on them, a fire burning in their inners, a rope pulling them one direction,
 them feeling like they're lost at sea. All of these actually are symbols. All of these actually are metaphors or symbolic representations of what's going on.
 Notice them, hear them, feel them and go, "Ah, that's a symbol." The inner attitude for that is how is consciousness showing itself right now.
 And then once you've heard it, like a stone or a rock on their heart or they're being pulled, ask them, "Okay, what does that being pulled look like?
 What does being lost at sea look like? What does the sea look like? What does the boat look like?"
 So we notice what they're saying. We're looking for images for symbols and we help them connect with them.
 If none of this happens, if none of what they say has any sort of visual representation or symbolism, and believe me, that's very, very, very, very difficult.
 You might have somebody who's hyper body oriented, but even they have to often use symbolic language.
 If there is nothing like that happening, ask them if this emotional sensation had a size or a shape, what would it be? What does it look like?
 That's the conversion. We don't know what this will be. We don't know if this is a person, more like phantom dialogue, we don't know if this is more like a part.
 "Oh, this is me when I was younger." We don't know any of that. It might be a stone, it might be a fire, it might be a water slide.
 We have to find out. But what we want to help them is to really get clear on this symbol by asking questions like, "Oh, what is this symbol made of? What material is it made of? What color does it have? What is the sound it makes? What weight does it have?"
 And then, once you have the symbol established, use all the questions you've learned in the past weeks to ask questions.
 "Okay, what is this boat trying to do? Where is this boat trying to go? Where is it coming from? What started this fire? What is this fire burning on? What is this fire achieving for you?"
 Any type of questions to really stay within the symbolic realm of your partner and then creatively work with that. Try it out. Feel free.
 The more we can listen to people's inner world that is always, always, always, always symbolic, the more we can understand, "Oh, they are representing things in a certain way.
 They are showing things to us that consciousness is representing itself in a certain way, and we can interact with that."
 So that's what this week's practice is really about. Free conversion, creativity. Try it out. See if any of the parts questions work, if any of the phantom dialogue questions work.
