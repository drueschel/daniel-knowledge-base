# Transkript: 07 Two Types of Consciousness

**Datei:** `07_Two_Types_of_Consciousness.ts`  
**Sprache:** Englisch  

---

## Inhalt:

Generally you have two different types of people. People who either stabilize on
 the outward emotions or people who stabilize on the inward emotions. And when
 people stabilize in inward emotions, sadness and fear for example, then people
 have trouble coming in contact with their own personal power, with their might,
 with their strength, with their anger. And they loop around. Looping means
 staying stuck in a certain programming. They loop around being sad or being weak
 or being a victim. This side of the emotions map is really where victim
 consciousness lives if we are stuck in that. On the other side we have more the
 perpetrator consciousness. It is where we instead of blaming ourselves, we
 constantly blame others and attack others and are unable to reflect about our
 own faults and see fully what part we play in our own suffering. So being locked
 in either the left or the right is both a situation that doesn't bring us
 anywhere. In this work our goal is to balance these qualities naturally
 through allowing the emotional process to finish.
