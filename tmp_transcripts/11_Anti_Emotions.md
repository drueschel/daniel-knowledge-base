And this is the most important part of the emotions map. These are the anti-emotions.
 I said before, emotions are vibrations, are energy. When emotions can vibrate out, they
 create a storage. If that storage is particularly dense, it's almost like a black hole. There's
 so much mass, so much energy stored in there. That we call an anti-emotion. That is meaninglessness,
 helplessness, hopelessness, shame, fear of death. These are emotions with such a strong charge
 that they are so, so, so difficult to be processed and felt that the human psyche creates cover
 emotions to not process them. So a person might get angry all the time, but it's not an authentic
 anger just to not process the anti-emotions underneath it. Just to not process the shame
 behind it. And the same goes for the other side. Some people always start crying, crying, crying,
 but they really feel helpless and they're unable to process that helplessness fully.
 So as an emotional releaser, we really watch out that we can bring this person to witness
 and meet these really strong anti-emotions. Because just like a black hole has an event horizon
 where a lot of energy is leaking out, from the inside it's just condensed energy that doesn't
 let anything out. And the same goes for these anti-emotions. We meet those emotions and we're
 almost sucked up into this dense, dense energy. And in that moment that we witness that, we go
 through a kind of death process. We're dying, but luckily only a part of us is dying that isn't
 congruent with reality anymore. So it's a healthy death that we have to die over and over again
 to actualize ourselves, to become free of our old habit patterns and stuck loops.
