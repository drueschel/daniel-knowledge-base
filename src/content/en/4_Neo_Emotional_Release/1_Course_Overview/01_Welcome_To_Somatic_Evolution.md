# Welcome to Somatic Evolution

## Introduction to the Neo-Emotional Release Training
Welcome to **Somatic Evolution**, the Neo-Emotional Release training. Emotional releases is a global movement focused on training people worldwide how to hold space and facilitate deep emotional transformation in others. 

## The Mission
By doing this work, we can fundamentally change the way humans interact. Currently, society suffers from collective existential pathologies:
* We suppress our emotions
* We suppress our creativity
* We are misinformed about chronic illnesses

All of these factors suppress our human potential. By creating more permission for humanity to be themselves, we help create a planet of health and well-being. By joining this training, you are joining this global mission.

## The Power of Neo-Emotional Release (NER)
David Manning has spent the last 10 years developing incredible therapeutic skills capable of transforming psychological and chronic health issues in just a few minutes. 

Over the next 10 weeks, you will learn the foundational skills needed to create and guide powerful emotional processes in another human being. 

## Course Structure
The course is built progressively, with each week building upon the last:
* **Weekly Training Classroom:** A live online call with a teacher.
* **Exact Steps & Practice:** You will learn the exact steps of each technique and practice them.

This environment fosters your inner facilitator, believing in your innate capacity to hold space and transform others.
