# Introduction to Expressive Breathing

The Emotional Release Breath is a foundational breathing technique utilized in Neo-Emotional Release (NER) to deepen the process of feeling and processing emotions. By increasing our physiological and psychological capacity to witness our internal states, this breath creates a safe container for emotional processing and healing.

## The Concept of Containment
* **Increasing Capacity:** Conscious breathing increases the body's capacity to process and witness emotions. 
* **The Surface Area of Healing:** This quality is known as *containment*. Containment can be understood as the internal "surface area" on which our witnessing presence meets the emotion. The act of this meeting is where the actual processing and healing of the emotion occurs.

## The Importance of the Breath in Practice
* **Essential for Release:** This specific breath is a crucial tool in this modality. Without the ability to produce this breath—or guide a client into it—it is difficult to fully open up the energetic and emotional body for deeper release work.
* **Breaking Holding Patterns:** The active engagement of the vocal cords and the outward sigh helps to open up initial physical holding patterns, specifically those centered around expression and the throat.

## Exercise: The Emotional Release Breath
Try this practical exercise to experience the breath yourself:

1. **Inhale deeply.**
2. **Exhale with an audible sigh.** It is essential to let the sound resonate out fully. 
3. **Visualization:** Imagine you have been hiking up a Himalayan mountain all day, and you finally reach the summit. Breathe in the accomplishment, and as you exhale, let go with a deep, resonant sigh of relief. Alternatively, imagine finally sitting down on the couch after a long, exhausting day and just letting it all go.
