# Week 5 Demo: Resourcing

This demonstration illustrates how to help a client connect with and amplify internal resources within their body and emotions. The process involves identifying comforting sensations, deeply experiencing them, and using different sensory channels to anchor a sense of safety and support.

## Localizing the Resource
- **Identify Comfort:** Ask the client to locate a "sweet," comfortable, or nurturing sensation in their body. It is often easiest to start with a physical sensation.
- **Example Response:** The client might describe warmth in their chest and legs, along with a feeling of comfort and safety.

## Amplifying and Describing the Sensation
- **Give Space and Time:** Encourage the client to take deep breaths and simply allow the positive feelings to be present.
- **Verbalize the Experience:** Ask the client to describe the sensation in detail (e.g., feeling the chest expand, sensing warmth, coziness, and a "protected truth").

## Utilizing Sound for Resourcing
- **Internal Sounding:** Invite the client to imagine a sound that this comforting sensation might make, one that they find beneficial or soothing.
- **Vocalization:** Encourage them to slowly make the sound louder. Be aware that resistance might arise, which is normal.
- **Holding the Sound:** Have the client experiment with holding the vocalized sound for a duration, such as 20 seconds.
- **Observation:** Guide them to observe the shifts and changes happening within their body as they make the sound. They should continue sounding as long as it feels resourcing.

## Somatic Integration
- **Continuous Process:** Resourcing is part of an ongoing process of somatic integration that builds up through different emotional and physical layers during a session.
- **Multidimensional Experience:** By engaging sensations, emotions, and sounds, you build a complex, three-dimensional view of the somatic experience, deeply connecting the resource to the client's emotional state.
