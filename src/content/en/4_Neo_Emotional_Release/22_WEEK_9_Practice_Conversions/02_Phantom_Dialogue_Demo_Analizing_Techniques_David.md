# Phantom Dialogue: Demo Analyzing Techniques

## Key Concepts
- **Definition of Conversions:** The skill of supporting clients to translate somatic bodily sensations and defined emotions into visual symbols to unlock deeper therapeutic work.
- **Integration of Prior Competencies:** Conversions build cumulatively on fundamental techniques such as connected awareness, logging in, somatic integration, and emotion definement.
- **Modality Spectrum:** Encompasses Phantom Dialogue (projecting past or present relational figures), Parts Work (connecting affects to sub-personalities), and Free Conversion (tracking spontaneous subconscious symbols).
- **Session Framework:** Due to the added complexity of symbolic interaction, practical exercises in this phase extend to approximately 45 minutes.

## Summary
This module introduces the overarching framework of "Conversions"—a pivotal therapeutic skill enabling clients to translate abstract somatic contractions and affective charges into vivid visual or kinesthetic symbols. Conversions never bypass the foundational somatic arc; practitioners continue to begin by establishing connected awareness, logging in with the client, and navigating bodily sensations through somatic integration before naming the core emotion. Only after completing these grounding steps is the conversion process initiated to give feelings tangible form.

The framework outlines three primary forms of conversion explored in practical sessions: The first is the "Phantom Dialogue," where emotional tension is projected as a person from the client's past or present to vocalize unexpressed truths. The second is "Parts Work," attributing emotional states to distinct internal parts or younger versions of the client. Lastly, "Free Conversion" trains facilitators to recognize and work directly with symbols that arise spontaneously from the client's subconscious landscape without forcing them into relational categories. Due to this multi-layered inquiry, conversion exercises are structured around 45-minute practice containers.
