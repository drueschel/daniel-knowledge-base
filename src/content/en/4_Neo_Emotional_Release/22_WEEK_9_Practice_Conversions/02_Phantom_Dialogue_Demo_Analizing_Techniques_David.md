# Phantom Dialogue - Demo Analizing Techniques | David

## Key Concepts
- **Conversions Definition**: The skill of helping clients translate somatic sensations and emotions into visual symbols to facilitate deeper work.
- **Integration of Prior Skills**: Conversions build upon foundational skills like connected awareness, logging in, somatic integration, and defining emotions.
- **Types of Conversions**: Includes Phantom Dialogue (representing a person from the past or present), Parts Work (connecting emotions to specific internal versions of oneself), and Free Conversion (working with existing symbols in the client's inner world).
- **Session Structure**: Practice sessions involving conversions are extended to roughly 45 minutes to accommodate the additional steps.

## Summary
In this section, the course introduces the concept of conversions, a crucial skill designed to help clients translate their somatic sensations and defined emotions into visual symbols. This process builds upon the foundational techniques covered in previous modules, meaning practitioners will still start sessions with connected awareness, log in with the client, and guide them through somatic integration before moving into defining emotions. Only after these steps are completed does the new conversion process begin, transforming these feelings into workable visual or kinesthetic representations.

The introduction outlines three main types of conversions that will be explored in upcoming exercises. The first is "Phantom Dialogue," where an emotion is converted into a representation of a person from the client's life who might be communicating through these feelings. The second is "Parts Work," which connects emotions to specific internal "parts" or versions of the client, often from their past. Lastly, "Free Conversion" teaches practitioners how to pick up on and work with symbols that naturally arise in the client's inner world without needing to define them as a phantom or a part. Because this process is more involved, these practice exercises will take about 45 minutes each.
