# How to Use Conversions

## Key Concepts
- **Definition of Conversions:** The therapeutic process of translating somatic bodily sensations and vague emotions into tangible visual (or kinesthetic) symbols so clients can directly relate to and work with them.
- **Cumulative Skill Hierarchy:** Conversions do not replace previous somatic fundamentals; they build upon them: *1. Logging In (Connected Awareness) $\rightarrow$ 2. Sourcing Resistance/Issue $\rightarrow$ 3. Somatic Integration $\rightarrow$ 4. Definement of Emotion $\rightarrow$ 5. Conversion into Symbol*.
- **Phantom Dialogue:** Converting somatic charge into the projected presence of a significant person from the past or present who communicates through that emotional sensation.
- **Parts Work:** Converting emotions into inner aspects or younger sub-personalities (internal parts) that are seeking connection or expressing unmet needs.
- **Free Conversions:** Attuning to and directly engaging with whatever spontaneous metaphors, organic imagery, or symbolic representations already arise within the client's inner world.

## Summary
This orientation lecture introduces the third foundational phase of the Somatic Evolution training: working with Conversions. A conversion is the systematic skill of taking physical sensations and emotional charges from within the body and transforming them into visual symbols. While some clients operate primarily through kinesthetic channels, visual representations offer the highest clarity for most individuals, transforming overwhelming internal states into concrete counterparts that can be observed, questioned, and integrated.

It is strongly emphasized that conversions must never be attempted in isolation from somatic anchoring. In every session, practitioners continue to rely on the sequence mastered in earlier weeks: entering connected awareness (logging in), clarifying the client's current challenge or somatic resistance, tracking physical sensations through somatic integration, and defining the emotional core (definement). Only when the feeling is fully grounded in the body is the new step initiated: translating the defined feeling into a symbolic form.

The training outlines three primary modalities of conversion:
1. **Phantom Dialogue:** Translating bodily tension into a visualization of an influential person (e.g., parent, partner, or authority figure) to resolve unexpressed boundaries and relational dynamics.
2. **Parts Work:** Treating somatic sensations as younger or protective parts of the self to understand their specific motivations and underlying wounds.
3. **Free Conversions:** Following the intuitive flow of the client's subconscious imagery—such as shapes, animals, or elements—without forcing a pre-determined structure.

Because navigating symbolic dialogue and emotional unburdening requires deeper immersion, the allotted time for practical exercises in this phase is extended to approximately 45 minutes per session.
