# Exact Steps Masterstate

The "Master State" is the most powerful principle in healing, representing the ultimate expression of unconditional love. It is a foundational state of being that can take years to fully cultivate, but it enables practitioners to hold space for the most difficult emotional releases and traumas.

## Core Qualities of the Master State
- **Unbiased Space Holding:** Creating a container of safety where your own groundedness and safety transfer to the client.
- **Radical Acceptance:** Wanting the person to be exactly as they are in that moment—with their defenses, contractions, and misery—without trying to change them or fixate on their potential.
- **Zero Percent Responsibility:** You are completely detached from the client's healing outcome. You anchor within yourself rather than reaching out to "hold" something for them. 
- **Active Foundation:** It is not a passive or quiet state; you can still confront, converse, and interact with the client, but every action stems from the foundation of the Master State.
- **Outcome Independence:** You are fully in the present moment, completely detached from where the client is going or the results of the session. This prevents counter-transference and pressure.
- **Deep Compassion and Witnessing:** Feeling the client on all layers, including the parts they try to hide or feel ashamed of, and loving them through awareness.
- **Surrender:** The Master State requires surrendering to the process rather than controlling it, acting as an empty vessel for healing to unfold. 

## The Depth of the Master State
The power of your Master State correlates directly with the depth of your own healing journey and the "ripeness" of your being. It is about how deeply in touch you are with yourself, as that depth is what you mirror to your client. 

## State Testing
State testing is a subconscious phenomenon where individuals express unlovable or chaotic versions of themselves to see if they can be contained. It is simultaneously an ego defense (trying to prove they are unlovable) and a profound outcry for love and safety. 

- **Insanity Testing:** Exhibiting extreme or hysterical expressions to prove they are beyond help. When met with the Master State, their nervous system calms down.
- **Drama Testing:** Creating unnecessary conflict or panic to test the stability of the relationship.
- **Container Testing:** Subtle provocations to see if you can truly hold space for them without reacting defensively.
