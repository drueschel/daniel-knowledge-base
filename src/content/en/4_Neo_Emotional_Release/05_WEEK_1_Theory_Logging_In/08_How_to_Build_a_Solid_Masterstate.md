# How to Build a Solid Masterstate

Building a solid Masterstate is the foundational requirement for effectively guiding and supporting a client during a Neo-Emotional Release session. It is rooted in patience, presence, and profound unconditional love.

## Key Elements of the Masterstate

*   **Patience and Calmness:** Slow down your pace. Speak with a deeper, slower voice to help bring the client into their body and heart. There is no rush and no agenda to "fix" anything.
*   **Dropping into the Now:** Release your plans and concepts about what needs to happen. Anchor yourself in the present moment. While thoughts will inevitably arise, simply acknowledge them and return to the present.
*   **Unconditional Love:** If you cannot love or hold space for a client, do not treat them. Loving a client means witnessing them, empathizing, and accepting every part of them—including their shame, trauma, or "unacceptable" aspects.
*   **Non-Judgmental Witnessing:** The greatest gift you can offer is seeing the client's wounded parts without judgment or the desire to change them. This profound acceptance may be the first time they have ever experienced such safety.
*   **Self-Regulation:** Notice your own internal reactions to the client's process without acting on them. A true Masterstate allows you to hold your own process while maintaining focus on the client.
*   **Activating Support:** When entering the Masterstate, an internal shift occurs. Hidden resources activate, and the focus turns entirely away from you, enabling you to act as a solid, supportive container for the client.
*   **Trusting Your Intuition:** Trust that you will inherently know what to do when the time is right. Avoid overthinking or second-guessing your interventions.
