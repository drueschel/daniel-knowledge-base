# Fragmentation and Repression

Repression is the act of not allowing our emotions to vibrate outward, be felt, and be witnessed. This suppression mechanism not only traumatizes us but also consumes a significant amount of the body's energy. However, by creating a safe environment to process these blocked emotions, we can restore synchronization within the brain and return to a state of wholeness.

## The Cost of Repression
- **Energy Expenditure:** Repression is an active process that costs the body energy and calories. 
- **Neurological Impact:** When trauma is present, the brain networks connected to the traumatic event stop firing synchronously. Instead, they begin firing in an asynchronous pattern, which demands a higher level of energetic processing from the body.
- **Self-Traumatization:** By avoiding the natural flow and witnessing of emotions, we inadvertently traumatize ourselves, leading to a split in the psyche.

## The Purpose of Fragmentation
- **Protective Mechanism:** The body splits our psyche and creates a fragmented state as a protective measure.
- **Inability to Process:** This occurs when a specific aspect of reality or an overwhelming experience cannot be fully witnessed or processed by our pure awareness.
- **Dissociation:** In response, we tend to disconnect from the body, and the brain networks begin to split, storing this unprocessed charge in a frozen state.

## The Path to Wholeness
- **Creating Safety:** Healing begins by establishing an environment that is safe enough for individuals to reconnect with their difficult aspects and blocked emotions.
- **Witnessing the Charge:** In this safe space, the frozen, repressed energetic charge held in the body can finally be felt and witnessed.
- **Restoring Synchronization:** As the unprocessed aspect is fully witnessed, the brain's networks transition from their asynchronous state back into a synchronous firing pattern.
- **Becoming Whole:** This neurological integration allows the split psyche to merge, enabling us to literally become whole again.
