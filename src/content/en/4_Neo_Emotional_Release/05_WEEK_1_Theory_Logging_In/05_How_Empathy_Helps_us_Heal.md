# How Empathy Helps Us Heal

By creating safety, holding space, and resonating with another person's emotional state, we help them access and release their repressed emotions. This profound act of witnessing and co-regulation is not only deeply healing but also represents the purest form of love, allowing people to be exactly as they are without the need to change them.

## The Power of Resonance and Co-Regulation
- **The Deepest Craving:** The thing human beings crave most is to be witnessed and to resonate with one another.
- **Vibrational Matching:** When we match another person's vibration and hold space for them, we expand our own internal capacity, which in turn gives them the space to feel their own emotions.
- **Healing Together:** By vibrating with each other, we co-regulate and facilitate mutual healing. Empaths naturally have a highly developed capacity to resonate with others.

## Mirroring and the Nervous System
- **Neurological Impact:** Mirror neurons play a significant role in therapeutic settings. Good therapists are excellent at mirroring their clients.
- **The Risks of Mirroring:** Studies have shown that therapists treating clients with severe trauma (like PTSD) can neurologically take on aspects of their clients' trauma if they are exposed for too long without proper boundaries and self-regulation.

## The Art of Space Holding (The Master State)
- **The Ultimate Technique:** Holding space is simultaneously the most basic and the most advanced technique you can learn. In NER, this is often referred to as the "Master State."
- **The Prerequisite of Inner Work:** You can only hold space for emotions that you can access within yourself. Mastering your own energy system and emotions is essential to effectively holding space for others.

## The Purest Form of Love
- **Unconditional Acceptance:** True love is allowing a person to fully be themselves without wanting them to change.
- **Releasing Manipulation:** Often, in relationships or when confronting family, we try to manipulate or change the other person. Healing requires stepping back and simply witnessing them as they are.
- **Impacting the Collective:** Every time one person holds space for another, it creates more space within the collective net of repressed trauma, contributing to a broader healing effect.
