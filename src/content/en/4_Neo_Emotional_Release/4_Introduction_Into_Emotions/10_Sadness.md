# Sadness

Sadness is an inward, destabilizing emotion that requires us to be deeply in touch with ourselves to truly feel it, including grief. It emerges when we feel a loss of connection or as if our soul is losing a part of itself.

## Core Concepts

* **The Nature of Sadness:** Sadness is experienced as an inward movement, characterized by a feeling of destabilization. It requires deep internal connection to fully process grief and sadness.
* **Loss of Connection:** It plays a major role when we feel we are losing a piece of ourselves—whether that is an aspect of our reality or a connection to another person.
* **The Movement of Grief:** Sadness is the feeling of losing a connection. Conversely, being deeply moved (such as by a poignant movie) often signifies the release of sadness, representing reintegration and coming back together.
* **Friction and Identity:** Sadness arises from the friction between two parts of yourself that you identify with and desire connection with. When these parts fall apart, the resulting inner movement is experienced as sadness.
