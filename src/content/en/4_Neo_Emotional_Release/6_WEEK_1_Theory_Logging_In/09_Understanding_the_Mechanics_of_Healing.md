# Understanding the Mechanics of Healing

Healing fundamentally requires feeling; we cannot heal what we cannot feel. At its core, healing is the meeting of our conscious awareness with the parts of reality that are difficult to process. Within each of us lies a dimension that is already whole and complete. When this innate wholeness comes into contact with blocked emotions and old programming, it begins to dissolve and melt them away, allowing us to contain previously unbearable parts of ourselves.

## The Body's Inherent Intelligence
* **Self-Organization:** The body possesses a profound inherent intelligence and is constantly seeking to self-organize and return to balance.
* **Stepping Out of the Way:** Profound, life-changing shifts occur when we simply step out of the way and allow the body's natural magical process of healing to unfold.
* **Dissolving Resistance:** Meeting painful or traumatic parts with readiness allows difficult resistances—which previously kept a person stuck in negative loops—to finally move out of the way.

## The Role of the Therapist
* **Restabilization:** Therapists use their own healed aspects to restabilize the client. By embodying wholeness, the therapist supports the client in accessing their own repressed resources, which include hidden abilities, skills, emotions, and strength.
* **Bridging the Gap:** Clients are often stuck because they have split off certain energies (like the ability to nurture or personal power). Because the therapist does not have these strengths split off, they can offer the healed version of that energy to activate the client's latent resources.
* **Completing the Loop:** The therapist provides the missing link that allows the client's nervous system to complete its self-organization and move up in consciousness.

## Reclaiming Split-Off Resources
When a client is missing a crucial part of their expression, the therapist can invite them to reclaim it. 

**Guided Encouragement:**
* *"Show me your anger."*
* *"Take your power."*
* *"Take your right."*

By explicitly opening space for these repressed energies, the therapist stabilizes the client's system, allowing them to exit their stuck patterns and elevate their consciousness.
