# Exercise: Establishing Boundaries

This exercise demonstrates how to maintain your personal boundaries while empathically connecting with another person. It helps you recognize the subtle energetic shift between staying grounded within your own space and extending your awareness to someone else.

## The Exercise

- **Define Your Personal Space:** Extend your arms outwards in front of you with your elbows slightly bent. The distance your hands reach forms your natural boundary.
- **Form the Wall:** Keep your hands in this position, visualizing them as a protective wall or boundary.
- **Connect with the Space:** Feel the space between your body and the wall you have created. Take a moment to deeply connect with this sensation of being contained and safe within your own "kingdom."
- **Witness the Other:** Once you feel securely anchored within your boundary, look over at the other person. Attempt to witness them while staying rooted in your own space.
- **Notice the Pull:** Observe if you feel "sucked in" or if you start losing your sense of self in the other person.
- **Return to Self:** If you lose your sense of boundary, immediately shift your focus back to your hands. Re-establish the feeling of your own boundary before trying to witness the other person again.

## Objective
The goal is to find a balance between maintaining a strong sense of your own boundary and empathetically witnessing another human being. It teaches you to return to yourself whenever that balance is lost.
