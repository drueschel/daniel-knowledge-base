# Week 4 Practice Room: Breath

In the fourth week of the Somatic Evolution course, somatic integration is taken a step further by incorporating breathwork into the practice. Building upon the equalizing techniques from the previous week, this session introduces expressive breathing to process and release physical or emotional resistance.

## Core Concepts

*   **Initial Assessment and Somatic Integration:**
    *   Begin by identifying any discomfort or resistance in the body.
    *   Guide the client through standard somatic integration steps: locate the sensation and describe its qualities (using a descriptive list if helpful).
*   **The Equalizing Phase:**
    *   Ask the client if they can welcome the sensation. The goal is to achieve a genuine "yes."
    *   If they answer "no," enter the equalizing loop by asking, "Can you welcome that it is difficult?"
    *   Even if the answer remains "no" after a few attempts, it is perfectly fine to proceed to the breathing exercise.
*   **Expressive Breathing:**
    *   Instruct the client to breathe directly into the identified sensation (e.g., throbbing, tingling, tightness).
    *   Incorporate *expressive breathing* by encouraging them to audibly sigh on the exhale.
    *   If they initially could not welcome the sensation, breathing into the resistance serves as a tool to create space and shift the experience.
*   **Monitoring and Observation:**
    *   After the breathing exercise, monitor the changes. Ask the client what has shifted or changed about the sensation.
*   **The Feedback Loop:**
    *   Repeat the process with any new sensations that arise: localize, describe, welcome, and breathe.

## Exercise Highlight: Overcoming Resistance to Expression

Many people find it challenging to make sounds or be expressive with their physical and emotional content due to societal conditioning. If a client struggles with expressive breathing, use the following visualization:

> "Imagine coming home after a long, challenging day. You walk in, sit down on the couch, and let the difficulty of the day just drop away. You naturally let out a deep sigh of relief and letting go."

Encourage the client to try this natural sigh of relief, then check in to see what they notice in their body afterward.
