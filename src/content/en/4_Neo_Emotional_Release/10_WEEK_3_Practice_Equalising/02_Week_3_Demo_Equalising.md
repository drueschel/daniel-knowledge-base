# Week 3 Demo: Equalising

In this demonstration, we explore how to deepen the connection with a bodily sensation by guiding the client through allowing, trusting, and ultimately finding joy within the experience.

## Building Trust and Allowing
* **The First Step:** Simply feeling the sensation is the initial act of putting trust into the body.
* **Identifying Resistance:** The practitioner asks if the sensation feels like a resistance (e.g., connected to fear) or simply a sensation that can be witnessed.
* **The Core Question:** Ask, *"Can you allow this sensation to be there?"* Encourage the client to be completely honest about how their body responds to this question.
* **Practitioner Awareness:** As a practitioner, tune into your own body's response to the client's answer. Notice if their voice conveys trust and reliability, or if it indicates nervous system activation or shutdown.

## Expanding with Curiosity and Breath
* **Fostering Curiosity:** Ask the client, *"Can you be curious about this sensation?"* to shift the mental framework towards openness and exploration.
* **Breathwork for Expansion:** Guide the client to take deep breaths, exhale with a sigh, and feel the sensation expanding in the body with each breath.

## Introducing Joy (Equalising)
* **Finding Joy:** Ask, *"Can you also be joyful with this experience, or enjoy this sensation?"*
* **Shifting Proportions:** Inquire about the percentage of joy compared to other feelings (e.g., resistance). Often, simply bringing awareness to the joy causes it to increase.
* **Witnessing the Shift:** Guide the client to receive and monitor this joy as a new, responding bodily sensation. Observe how it connects to or expands beyond the original area of sensation.
