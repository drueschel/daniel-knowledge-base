# Week 3 Practice Room: Equalising

This week expands on the basic Somatic Integration process by introducing "Equalising," a powerful technique designed to help clients create space and genuine acceptance for sensations of resistance.

## Core Concepts & Process

- **Localizing and Describing (Review)**
  - Begin by guiding the client to focus on a sensation of resistance (e.g., an uncomfortable feeling like tightness or throbbing).
  - **Localize the sensation:** Ask, "Where is this resistance?"
  - **Describe the sensation:** Ask, "How would you describe it?"

- **Equalizing the Sensation (New Step)**
  - Mirror the client's exact terminology to validate their experience (e.g., "this throbbing, tingling tightness").
  - **First Question:** Ask, "Can you allow this sensation to be there?"
  - **If the answer is Yes:** Provide the client with ample time to process the sensation in silence, then proceed to the monitoring phase.
  - **If the answer is No:** Shift the focus of acceptance by asking, "Can you welcome that you have a resistance to this sensation?"
  - **If the answer is still No:** Create even more space by asking, "Can you welcome that it is difficult for you to allow this sensation?"

- **Working with Persistent Resistance**
  - Continue offering gentle variations of the equalizing question until the client reaches a point of acceptance (a "yes"). Welcoming the resistance itself shifts the internal dynamic.
  - If the resistance remains highly persistent and unyielding, avoid forcing the process. Instead, redirect the client to a grounding exercise, such as a body scan, to re-establish safety and presence.

- **Monitoring and Cycling**
  - Once a point of acceptance ("yes") is achieved and the sensation is equalized, ask the client to monitor the internal shift: "What has changed?" or "What are you perceiving now?"
  - After identifying the change or the new sensation, restart the therapeutic cycle: localize the new or shifted sensation, describe it, and repeat the equalizing process.

## Exercise Structure

- Conduct this cycle continuously for 15 minutes.
- Guide the client through as many cycles of localizing, describing, equalizing, and monitoring as the time allows.
- Conclude the session with a dedicated feedback segment to integrate the experience.
