# Week 6 Practice: Resourcing and Expansion with Sound

In the final week focused on somatic integration, this exercise builds upon resourcing by introducing a new technique: expanding a positive sensation or resource using sound. The goal is to help clients deepen their capacity to feel and hold positive sensations.

## Core Concepts

- **Establish the Resource:** Invite the client to think of a resource and identify the corresponding physical sensation. Localize it in the body, describe it in detail, and monitor any changes that occur.
- **The "Welcome" Question:** Ask the client if they can welcome or enjoy the sensation. 
  - If the answer is no, scale back the request: "Can you welcome half of it?", "Can you welcome 10% of it?", or simply, "Can you welcome that it is difficult right now?"
  - The objective is to guide the client toward some form of a "yes" or acceptance.
- **Overcoming Resistance:** If a client remains stuck in a "no," be prepared to shift strategies. You can return to deep breathing, guide a body scan, or use a somatic intervention to create a shift before returning to the exercise.

## Exercise: Expanding with Sound

Once the client can welcome the resource, use sound to expand the sensation:
- **Determine the Pitch:** Ask, *"If this expansive, joyful sensation were to vibrate, would it make a high or low sound?"*
- **Internal Amplification:** Have the client hear this sound internally, gradually making it louder and louder.
- **External Vocalization:** Invite the client to vocalize the sound out loud and hold it for 20 seconds. 
- **Observe and Loop:** After the vocalization, monitor what changes. Does the sensation expand? Does a new sensation emerge? Return to the steps of localizing, describing, and monitoring, and continue the cycle.

> **Note for Future Sessions:** This concludes the specific focus on somatic integration. In future sessions, the four foundational steps—localizing, describing, monitoring, and equalizing—will be standard practice.
