# Total Surrender: Full Guided Release

This guided release process is designed to help you drop into the present moment by letting go of expectations and welcoming whatever arises. The core of this practice is surrendering the need to change, fix, or heal anything, allowing you to fully inhabit your body and emotions exactly as they are right now.

## 1. Preparing the Space and Body
- **Settle In:** Find a relaxed posture in a safe space where you can make noise and won't be distracted.
- **Let Go of Expectations:** Put aside any preconceived ideas of what an emotional release should look like.
- **Anchor in Awareness:** Slowly sink your attention into your body. Shift your focus back and forth between your upper and lower body before allowing your awareness to rest on the body as a whole.

## 2. Welcoming Resistance
- **Notice the Mind:** Observe if your mind resists being fully present (e.g., wondering when the exercise will be over).
- **Locate it Physically:** Feel where this resistance lives in your body—whether it's tightness, lightness, or any sensation in the chest, shoulders, face, or stomach.
- **Breath and Sound:** Take deep breaths in through the mouth and exhale with an audible sigh. Use your voice to give the resistance permission to be there, and then allow it to slowly dissipate.

## 3. The Core Surrender
Cancel all plans of the mind and surrender to these profound truths:
- **There is nothing to fix.**
- **There is nothing to heal.**
- **There is no one to become.**
- **There is nowhere else to be.**

Simply being here in this moment is all your soul, heart, and body want.

## 4. Emotional Naming and Embodiment
- **Identify the Emotion:** Focus on any lingering physical sensation and feel what emotion resonates most with it (fear, anger, sadness, shame, guilt, joy).
- **Give Permission:** Welcome the emotion directly. Tell it: *"You're welcome here. I'm here for you. I see you."*
- **Physicalize the Feeling:** Allow the rest of your body to vibrate with the emotion. Ask yourself what facial expression this emotion wants to make, and physically express it to let the energy move through you.
