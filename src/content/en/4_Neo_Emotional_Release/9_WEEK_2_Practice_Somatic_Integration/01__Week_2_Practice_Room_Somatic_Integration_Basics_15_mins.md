# Week 2 Practice Room: Somatic Integration Basics

In week two of the Somatic Evolution course, we move beyond simply "landing" and introduce the core process of Somatic Integration. This session focuses on guiding clients through a sensation-based process to locate, describe, and observe changes in their bodily sensations or areas of resistance.

## Core Concepts

- **Establishing the Foundation:** Continue practicing the "landing" process, which includes self-awareness, connected awareness, establishing the master state, and guiding the client through a foundational body scan.
- **The Three Steps of Somatic Integration:**
  - **Localizing:** Ask the client where they perceive sensations or resistance (such as physical or emotional challenges). If multiple areas are mentioned, ask which one they feel most drawn to.
  - **Describing:** Encourage the client to articulate the sensation.
    - Start with open questions (e.g., "How would you describe the sensation?").
    - If they struggle to find the words, offer descriptive options (e.g., achy, frozen, pounding, throbbing) or opposing contrasts (e.g., spacious vs. tight, hot vs. cold).
    - **Crucial Tip:** Mirror the exact words the client uses to describe their experience. This makes the process easier for both of you and builds deep rapport.
  - **Monitoring:** Allow a moment of silence for the client to stay with the sensation. Afterward, ask what has changed or what they perceive now in their body.

## Practice Structure

- **Logging In:** Begin the session by guiding the client through the standard logging-in and body scan exercises.
- **Cycle of Integration:** Continuously loop through the steps of Localizing, Describing, and Monitoring as sensations shift, evolve, or move to new areas of the body.
- **Feedback Exercise:** Conclude the 15-minute practice session with structured reflection—4 minutes of feedback for the client, followed by 2 minutes of feedback for the facilitator.
