# Week 5 Practice Room: Resourcing

In this session of the Somatic Evolution course, the focus expands on Somatic Integration by introducing the concept of "Resourcing." The primary goal is to guide clients in identifying and connecting with positive resources—such as a person, an animal, a place, a memory, a belief, or an inner strength—and to mindfully explore the physical sensations these resources evoke in the body.

## The Resourcing Process

*   **Identifying the Resource:** Begin by instructing the client to think of a positive resource. It can be anything that is positive for them and activates a pleasant feeling or sensation.
*   **Locating the Sensation:** Once the resource is identified, ask the client to notice what they feel in their body when they think about it.
*   **Somatic Integration:** Follow the standard Somatic Integration flow by localizing, describing, and monitoring the sensations as they change.
*   **Exploration Phase:** Stay in this basic flow for about five minutes to deeply explore the sensations of the resource and observe what happens as the client stays with them.

## Expanding the Sensation

After the initial five minutes of exploration, invite the client to deepen their experience.

**Key Question:** *"Can you expand this sensation, or can you enjoy it?"*

### If the Client Says "Yes"
*   Give them 20 to 30 seconds to simply experience and expand the sensation.
*   Resume the monitoring process: Ask where the expansion is happening and have them describe what they feel.
*   Continue the cycle of localizing, describing, monitoring, and expanding for the remainder of the exercise (approximately 20 minutes).

### If the Client Says "No" (Equalizing)
*   It is crucial to understand that for some clients, especially those with trauma histories, experiencing pleasurable sensations can be highly challenging. Do not be surprised if they answer "no" to enjoying or expanding the feeling.
*   If this occurs, apply the principle of equalizing to help them find a tolerable level of the sensation.

**Exercise / Equalizing Questions:**
*   *"Can you welcome half of it?"*
*   *"Can you welcome 10% of it?"*
*   *"Can you welcome that it is difficult to allow this sensation?"*

Once you reach some form of a "yes," allow them time to sit with that smaller percentage or with the difficulty itself. Proceed with the standard monitoring and localizing process. The ultimate aim is to gently invite positivity and help the client gradually build the capacity to enjoy what feels good.
