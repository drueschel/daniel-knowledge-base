# Elements of Release

Neo-Emotional Release (NER) utilizes three core elements to facilitate profound emotional shifts. By combining touch, breath, and verbal guidance, NER works dynamically to address underlying emotional blockages and directly communicate with the consciousness behind our emotions, distinguishing it from purely physical therapeutic approaches.

## The Three Core Elements of Release

*   **Touch:** Applying the correct pressure to specific areas or "doorways" in the body confronts the individual with the emotional charge stored in that physical location, triggering a release or shift.
*   **Breath:** Intentional breath work significantly increases the body's capacity to process and integrate complex emotions.
*   **Verbal Guidance:** Saying the right thing at precisely the right time acts as an emotional "needle" (similar to an acupuncture needle). Hitting the right spot verbally can cause profound releases.

## Addressing the Root Cause

While many physical therapies (like osteopathy or massage therapy) manipulate the physical body to restore structural alignment, they often remain on the physical dimension. NER goes deeper by:

*   Addressing the underlying emotional contractions that *cause* the physical misalignments.
*   Directly communicating with the consciousness residing within the emotion.
*   Treating the root cause of disease and negativity in life, rather than just alleviating physical symptoms.
