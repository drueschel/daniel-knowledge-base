# Welcome to Somatic Evolution

Neo-Emotional Release is a global movement dedicated to training individuals worldwide to hold space and facilitate profound emotional transformation. By developing these skills, we aim to fundamentally change human interaction and address the collective suffering and existential pathologies prevalent in society.

## The Core Problem: Suppression

Society currently faces widespread suppression that limits our human potential. This manifests in several ways:
- **Suppressed Emotions:** We are often taught to hide or ignore how we truly feel.
- **Suppressed Creativity:** Our natural drive to express ourselves is frequently stifled.
- **Misinformation on Chronic Illness:** There is a lack of understanding regarding the connection between emotional suppression and physical health.

## The Mission of Somatic Evolution

The ultimate goal of this training is to cultivate a healthier, more authentic world. By participating in this training, you are contributing to:
- **Creating Permission:** Fostering environments where individuals feel safe and permitted to be their authentic selves.
- **Global Healing:** Helping to build a planet centered on health and well-being through deep emotional transformation.
