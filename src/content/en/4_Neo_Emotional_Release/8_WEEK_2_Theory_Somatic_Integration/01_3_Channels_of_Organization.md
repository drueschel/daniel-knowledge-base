# 3 Channels of Organization

The human body and psyche are governed by a continuous, self-organizing principle that is always working to heal and regenerate. In a Neo-Emotional Release (NER) session, we can access this natural healing intelligence through three distinct channels of physical awareness. By tracking these channels, we can see where healing is happening and guide the process more effectively.

## 1. The Sensational Channel
This is the most primal, baseline channel of our physical awareness. It develops first and serves as the rudimentary building blocks of our experience.
- Encompasses our entire felt sense throughout the body.
- Includes physical sensations like pain, pulsing, itching, tingling, pressure, heaviness, lightness, burning, heat, cold, contraction, and expansion.
- Awareness of this channel can wake up parts of the body that were previously numb.

## 2. The Emotional Channel
Emotions are also felt as sensations, but they carry a different frequency, bandwidth, and "juice" that touches us on a deeper, more personal level.
- Closer to our soul and more intimately connected to our sense of self.
- Typically felt along the center line of the body (e.g., the throat, the heart, or the gut).
- Includes states like fear, anger, sadness, guilt, and shame.

## 3. The Soul Vision Channel
This is the most highly developed channel, representing the dreamscape and the subconscious. It taps into a higher intelligence that provides highly specific information about our wounds and healing.
- Experiences arrive as images, symbols, memories, colors, people, animals, and places.
- Offers deep insights into a trauma's origin, such as how old it is or who it relates to.
- Enables "phantom dialogues" to heal relational wounds by interacting with an image of another person.
- Animals can appear to symbolize feared traits or offer empowering resources and qualities.

## Working with the Channels in Session
A core skill in NER is recognizing which channel a client naturally prefers and which they may be avoiding.
- **Balancing Channels:** A highly psychic client who defaults to Soul Vision may need to be grounded in the Sensational Channel. Conversely, a very physical client might need encouragement to open their "third eye" to symbolism.
- **Fluid Navigation:** Throughout a session, you will fluidly switch between channels. If movement stops on the emotional level, you might shift to Soul Vision for more information, and then return to the Sensational Channel to ground that new insight into the physical body.
- **The Rule of Depth:** The deeper the roots go into the Sensational Channel, the higher and more safely you can explore the Soul Vision Channel in a grounded manner.
