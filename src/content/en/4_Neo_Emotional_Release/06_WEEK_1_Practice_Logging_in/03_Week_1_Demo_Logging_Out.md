# Week 1 Demo: Logging Out

The "Logging Out" phase of a session is crucial for gently bringing clients back to their physical bodies after a deep journey into their subconscious. The goal is to provide a smooth, unhurried transition back to reality, avoiding any shock or abruptness.

## The Process of Logging Out
* **Sensory Reorientation:** Guide the client to reconnect with their five senses. Ask them to notice smells, sounds in the room, tastes, and invite the sensation of touch back to their fingertips and toes.
* **Intuitive Movement:** Encourage gentle wiggles and intuitive stretches, trusting that the body knows what it needs.
* **Pacing:** Allow the client to take their time. There are no strict time requirements—give them the space to let "the dust settle."
* **Visual Re-entry:** Coming back to light and opening the eyes can be intense. Suggest covering the eyes initially. Let them know they don't have to look at the screen immediately; they can focus on an object in the room to reorient themselves first.

## Handling Feedback and Integration
* **Be Gentle with Analysis:** Right after a deep release, clients may feel vulnerable. This is usually not the right time for deep analysis or heavy feedback.
* **Physical Needs:** Allow space for basic needs like getting a glass of water or taking a bathroom break, especially after longer sessions.
* **Setting Expectations:** Explain that the session has opened a doorway to the subconscious. They may experience vivid dreams, insights, or "downloads" in the following days. Encourage them to take notes of these occurrences.
* **Holding the Container:** Let the client know they can reach out to share their experiences in the days following the session. Following up with them provides a supportive container for their ongoing integration.
