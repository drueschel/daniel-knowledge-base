# Week 1 Practice Room: Logging In

In this foundational exercise of the Somatic Evolution course, you will learn the essential steps to begin a session. The focus is on establishing connected awareness, anchoring the "master state," and guiding the client into a grounded, relaxed state through a process called "logging in" or a body scan. This sequence will become second nature as the starting point for all future sessions.

## The Three Steps of Starting a Session

### 1. Self-Awareness (Facilitator)
- **Pause and Ground:** Close your eyes, take a deep breath, and sigh to relax your body.
- **Ease into Presence:** Allow yourself up to 5 minutes to fully arrive and ease into the session.
- **Anchor within:** Ensure you feel completely present with yourself before turning your attention outward.

### 2. Connected Awareness
- **Expand the Field:** Transition smoothly from pure self-awareness to being present with the client.
- **Maintain Boundaries:** Observe how you connect. If you find your attention completely leaving yourself and moving entirely to the other person, gently return your focus back to yourself.
- **Find the Balance:** The goal is to reach a space where you can simultaneously be with yourself *and* with the client without losing your center.

### 3. Guiding the Client (Body Scan)
- **Log In:** From a state of connected awareness, guide the client through a gentle body scan.
- **Move Awareness:** Instruct them to move their attention through their physical body.
- **Non-Judgmental Relaxation:** Encourage them to simply observe and relax without judging what they feel.
- *Note: This entire sequence (Steps 1-3) should take up to 15 minutes.*

## The Feedback Process

After the exercise, engaging in structured feedback helps both the facilitator and the client integrate the experience and improve.

- **Client Feedback (4 Minutes):**
  - Share freely about the experience.
  - Highlight what felt good and what was not as helpful.
  - Focus on your subjective, felt experience rather than criticizing the facilitator. (Even an expertly facilitated session can feel uncomfortable).
- **Facilitator Reflection (2 Minutes):**
  - Ask clarifying questions (e.g., "I didn't fully understand this part, can you explain?").
  - Ask for constructive input: "What would have supported you more?"
  - Share what you learned from the process and what you are taking away to become a better facilitator.
