# The Relationship Between Trauma and Emotions

In Neo-Emotional Release (NER), we expand our understanding of trauma beyond dramatic events. By reframing trauma as "imprints" or blocked emotions, we recognize that everyone carries these marks from daily life. These imprints are simply emotions that we couldn't finish feeling, waiting for resolution within a body that is naturally constantly trying to heal.

## The Buddhist Concept of Sankara
The Buddhist concept of *Sankara* describes how imprints are created with three different intensities:
- **Line on the water:** A mild wound, such as receiving a bad look, that vanishes in a second.
- **Line on the sand:** An event that affects you all day, like a minor argument, but washes away by the evening.
- **Line on rock:** A deep, lasting incision that can impact a lifetime, often associated with severe trauma.

## Shock Trauma vs. Developmental Trauma
- **Shock Trauma:** An event that overwhelms the system in a single moment (e.g., a car accident). To protect you, the system shuts down the mind's witnessing capability—often described as leaving the body or dissociating. This allows instinctual biological survival mechanisms ("the me") to take over and get you to safety without conscious thought.
- **Developmental Trauma:** Accumulates over a period of time. A "line on the water" repeated endlessly becomes a "line on the sand," which eventually deepens into a "line on rock."

## Redefining Trauma as Imprints
- **Moving Beyond Drama:** The word "trauma" (Greek for wound) often conjures highly dramatic associations. NER prefers the term **imprint** because everyday life constantly imprints us.
- **Blocked Emotions:** An imprint is essentially a blocked emotion. When the mind enters survival mode, it prevents the emotional aspect from being processed and stores it within the body.
- **Seeking Resolution:** Imprints are experiences we couldn't finish feeling (like a childhood promise that was never fulfilled). The whole system will continuously try to find a resolution. The body naturally tries to self-organize and heal, but the mind and ego often obstruct this inherent capacity.
- **The Mind-Body Matrix:** Emotions and thoughts are deeply interwoven. Every thought has an emotion attached to it, and every emotion generates thoughts. They are blended together seamlessly.

## Reflections and Exercises
During this session, participants are invited to reflect on their own experiences with different intensities of imprints. Consider asking yourself:
- *What is an example in your life of a "line on the water" imprint that you felt and immediately let go of?*
- *Can you identify a "line on the sand" imprint, such as a frustration that lingered for a day before resolving?*
- *Consider how shock trauma induces dissociation—have you ever felt your instinctual self take over to protect you while your conscious presence detached?*
