# How Trauma Shapes the Psyche

As we grow up, our natural state of open emotional expression often becomes restricted by societal and relational demands, leading to the creation of a "role ego" and the fragmentation of our authentic selves. Traumatic events further this fragmentation by causing us to compartmentalize unbearable emotions and memories, creating internal loops that seek resolution through life's triggers.

## The Openness of Childhood
- Babies exist in a highly receptive, open state (theta/delta brainwaves), akin to a blank canvas.
- Emotions flow naturally in and out without shame or hesitation—when a baby is angry or sad, it expresses it fully.

## The Creation of the Role Ego
- As we mature, we often create a "role ego"—an external identity or wall to be someone else for others, rather than our authentic selves.
- This creates a split in the psyche, fragmenting our identity into various parts tailored for our parents, our partners, and society.
- The imprinting process deepens these fragments every time we suppress our true selves to fit into these constructed roles.

## The Anatomy of Trauma
- A traumatic event is a shock or emotional spike that disrupts our normal state of consciousness.
- When an experience becomes too unbearable to witness, we dissociate ("leave our body") and create an internal container.
- This container holds the memories, emotions, and consciousness of the event, isolating it from our waking awareness.

## The Holographic Nature of Fragmentation
- These fragmented parts are holographic in nature; even the smallest fragment contains the entirety of the experience (emotions, memory, thoughts, consciousness).
- They act almost as separate entities within us, such as an inner child holding onto a specific pain.
- **Exercise Highlight:** When asked to "point to where you feel it" in the body, we locate and open this internal container, allowing the trapped emotion and fragmented part to be acknowledged and unboxed.

## Looping and Triggers
- If these fragmented containers remain closed, the trapped energy continuously loops, seeking resolution.
- This looping causes us to unconsciously repeat the past—making the same mistakes or attracting the same damaging situations because the body is trying to find a resolution.
- **Triggers:** When a life event carries a similar emotional frequency to what is trapped inside the container, we become triggered.
- Triggers are actually life's way of helping us reintegrate; they present opportunities to open the container, release the emotion, and become whole again.
