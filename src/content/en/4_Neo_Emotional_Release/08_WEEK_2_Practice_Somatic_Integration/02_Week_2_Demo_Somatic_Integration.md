# Week 2 Demo: Somatic Integration

This session provides a practical demonstration of somatic integration, guiding the client to connect with and process physical sensations within the body.

## Core Concepts

* **Focusing on Sensation:** Begin by bringing awareness to the body and choosing a specific sensation to focus on.
* **Localization and Observation:** Pinpoint the exact location, size, and boundaries of the sensation. Take time to deeply observe and connect with this specific area.
* **Witnessing the Channels:** Observe what arises through different channels—sensations, emotions, and thoughts—without judgment or interference.
* **Detailed Description:** Use adjectives to fully describe the sensation to deepen your conscious connection to it.
* **Monitoring Changes:** Notice how the sensation shifts over time. Observe if it expands, contracts, moves, or changes in intensity.
* **Tracking Connections:** Check if the sensation remains localized or if it connects and spreads to other parts of the body, allowing the energy to integrate.

## The Process in Action

During the demonstration, the client initially felt a strong, pinching energy localized in their lower belly. By sending breath to the area and sustaining observation, the sensation transformed into a softer, calmer feeling. Eventually, the energy expanded into the head and spread throughout the body, creating warmth and a sense of being energized rather than restricted.
