# Demo: Somatic Protection

In this demonstration, we observe how profound healing occurs when a client is guided to accept and welcome a part of themselves they have been deeply resisting. By embracing the resisted version of oneself, the internal war ceases, allowing life force to return and the individual to move toward wholeness and integration.

## The Cost of Resistance
- We are often composed of multiple layers of protection and resistance to reality.
- **Depression as Resistance:** Depression can be understood as an embodied process of multiple layers of resistance to life, often stemming from childhood or transgenerational trauma.
- Resisting life causes the body to become numb and shut down, which demands significant energy and resources from the brain to maintain presence.

## The Power of Welcoming
- You have everything to gain by accepting the versions of yourself that you usually reject. 
- When you stop fighting and being at war with yourself, you free up trapped life force, leading to feelings of lightness, clarity, and happiness.
- Finding one positive aspect of the resisted part (e.g., recognizing that a "sick" or "weak" part teaches compassion) can shift the entire somatic experience.

## Navigating Deep Resistance
When guiding someone to accept what they are resisting, they might encounter further layers of resistance. If this happens, you can soften the approach:
- **Welcome the struggle:** Ask, *"Can you accept and welcome that you are struggling with this right now?"*
- **Fractional acceptance:** Ask, *"Can you welcome just 1% of it? Can you welcome 2% of it?"*

## Exercise: Welcoming the Resisted Self
If you are struggling with a part of yourself, try this simple but powerful prompt:
- *Can you welcome this version of yourself in for a moment?*
- *Can you enjoy her/his/their presence? What is one thing you find enjoyable or valuable about this part?*
- Allow any resulting feelings of compassion or relief to expand physically into your chest, shoulders, and arms.

*Homework for clients:* Encourage them to spend more time enjoying the company of the version of themselves they are resisting.
