# Somatic Evolution: What is an Emotion?

An emotion is fundamentally "energy in motion." It can be best described as a vibration with a distinct beginning, middle, and end. When we are fully open to the emotional charges within our bodies, we can host this energy and allow it to naturally pass through and vibrate out.

## The Process of Emotional Repression
*   **Interrupted Flow:** When we are unable to let an emotion pass through, the natural process of vibrating out is interrupted.
*   **Energy Storage:** The unexpressed emotion becomes repressed and is stored as a concentrated energy within the body.
*   **Location and Frequency:** The location where this energy is stored correlates directly with the specific frequency of the stress that caused it.
*   **Unique Stress Signatures:** Different types of stress (e.g., arguing with a loved one, running late, or suppressing one's truth) carry distinct frequencies and manifest in corresponding parts of the body.

## The Manifestation of Chronic Conditions
*   **Organ Correspondence:** Unprocessed emotional topics have specific frequencies that often correspond to particular organs (e.g., heart, liver, or lung diseases).
*   **Stagnant Energy:** When stored, the energy is no longer in motion. The body's ecosystem stabilizes around this blockage, causing contractile tissue to literally hold on and contract.
*   **Living in Resistance:** The body becomes "armed" to live in resistance to a reality it could not fully process.
*   **The Unfinished Feeling:** A blocked emotion is simply a feeling we couldn't finish experiencing; it is a lingering energy that ultimately just wants to complete its cycle and vibrate out.
