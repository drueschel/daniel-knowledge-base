# Introduction to Definement

Definement is a core technique in Neo-Emotional Release designed to deepen the emotional process by accurately identifying and naming the emotion being felt. By giving a precise label to a physical sensation or energy, practitioners can help clients bring repressed emotions to the surface and facilitate a profound somatic release. 

## The Neuroscience of Definement

To understand why this technique is so effective, it is helpful to look at how the brain processes experiences:
*   **The Limbic System:** This is where our emotions are rooted and regulated.
*   **The Neocortex:** This is the thinking, cognitive part of the brain that tries to understand what is happening.
*   **Brain Synchronization:** When the neocortex successfully identifies and names the emotion the limbic system is experiencing (e.g., recognizing "this is anger"), the brain synchronizes with the body. This understanding instantly deepens the emotional process.

## How to Apply the Definement Technique

The process is remarkably simple but requires intuition and attunement to the client's responses. 

*   **Ask Direct Questions:** Begin by asking the client to describe the physical sensation (e.g., "What are you feeling?"). Then, offer potential emotions as questions: "Is it fear?" or "Is it anger?"
*   **Observe the Somatic Response:** Pay close attention to how the body reacts. When you correctly identify the emotion, there is often a sudden, visible shift—the emotion rises to the surface immediately. 
*   **Define the Source:** You can take definement even deeper by identifying the origin of the emotion. Ask questions like, "Is this from your mother?" or "Is this from your father?" Accurate identification will further intensify the emotional release.

## Transforming the Emotional Experience

Once the emotion is defined, the next step is to guide the client into full allowance. 
*   **Remove Resistance:** When a client stops resisting an emotion like anger, it can transform from something overwhelming into an energy that feels "juicy," alive, and powerful. 
*   **Change the Perspective:** Use creative metaphors to help the client welcome the feeling. For instance, suggesting they "coat the energy in chocolate" like a strawberry can help them experience the emotion as a delicious, vital life force rather than something to fear. 
*   **Welcome Doubts:** If the client experiences doubt or guilt about feeling their own energy, simply invite them to welcome the doubt as well, allowing the process to unfold naturally.
