# Bringing It Together: Verbal Principles

In this lesson, we begin to assemble the foundational techniques used in real-life Neo-Emotional Release sessions. Approximately 90% of the prompts utilized by a therapist fall into four main categories. These core verbal principles are designed to guide the client deeper into their somatic experience.

## Core Verbal Prompts

The primary prompts serve either as an invitation to experience or as a way to define the emotion:

*   **"Can you allow it?"**
    *   This is the foundational verbal principle and serves as an invitation.
    *   *Alternative phrasing:* "Can you enjoy that aliveness?"
*   **"What do you feel?"**
    *   This prompt focuses on *Definement*. It helps the client identify and articulate their current emotional or physical state.
*   **"Where do you perceive it [in your body]?"**
    *   This is a crucial question for digging deeper into the somatic experience. It further defines the emotion by locating it physically within the body.

## The Purpose of These Prompts

These questions function dynamically in a session: one set acts as a gentle invitation to simply be with the sensation, while the other actively defines the emotion by clarifying its nature and its physical location in the body.
