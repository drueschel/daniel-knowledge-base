# Definement: Theory and Examples

Definement is the process of getting to know ourselves on a profound level, enabling us to clearly articulate and interact with what is happening internally. It goes far beyond mental understanding, encompassing all levels of our system to bring clarity to the unknown or suppressed parts of our psyche.

## The Multidimensional Nature of Definement
Definement is not limited to cognitive explanation. It can be expressed and experienced through various channels:
*   **Embodiment:** Expressing internal states through movement or dance.
*   **Vocalization:** Using singing or resonance to match an internal feeling.
*   **Visualization:** Drawing or imagining elements that provide a deeper understanding.
*   **Breath and Memory:** Finding specific breathing patterns or reclaiming memories connected to a current emotion.

## Navigating the Unknown and Overwhelm
*   **Diving into the Unknown:** Definement often means exploring parts of ourselves that lack clarity or have been pushed aside.
*   **Untangling Overwhelm:** When facing a mixture of emotions (e.g., stress from work, anger from a relationship, anxiety about the future), definement helps distinguish and isolate these feelings.
*   **Focusing on One Element:** In emotional release, we start by focusing on a single, localized feeling in the body (e.g., "What do you feel in your chest right now?") to begin the definement process.

## The Role of Resonance in Definement
Resonance is the key indicator of whether we are accurately defining an experience. It manifests as a rise or shift in energy when a specific point is hit:
*   **Positive Resonance:** An immediate connection and excitement ("Yes, that's exactly it!"). The body actively affirms the definition.
*   **Negative Resonance:** Defensiveness, anger, or strong resistance. This trigger is itself a form of resonance that can be explored.
*   **No Resonance:** A neutral or bored reaction. This indicates the current track is not leading to a deeper definition and a new approach is needed.

## Definement as a Starting Point
*   **Opening the Door:** Definement is the basis and introduction to deeper emotional release work. It provides new information, reframing, and understanding.
*   **Pathway to Conversion:** Achieving clarity through definement opens the door to deeper techniques, such as conversions (which will be covered in later weeks).

## The Facilitator's Role
*   **Mirroring and Reframing:** Facilitators use their own resonance to feel what the client is experiencing and offer new framing based on their own resolved experiences.
*   **The Importance of Inner Work:** A facilitator can only guide a client as deep as they have gone themselves. Personal definement is crucial for effective facilitation.
*   **Navigating Uncertainty:** Even when the direction of a session is unclear, the facilitator can map out possibilities and follow the flow of life force and excitement to guide the definement process.

## Overcoming Resistance and Looping
*   **The "Clever" Client:** Sometimes clients mentally understand their issues but avoid feeling them deeply, leading to defensiveness ("I already know this").
*   **Breaking the Loop:** If a problem remains unsolved despite mental understanding, it means true definement hasn't occurred. Definement helps bypass mental looping to connect with the underlying frustration or trauma.
*   **Addressing Deep Frustration:** Frustration often stems from a lack of trust that an issue can be solved. Definement helps mirror this frustration back, exploring its roots (e.g., past experiences of failure or lack of support) to restore trust.
