# What is Emotional Release?

Emotional Release is a facilitation process that empowers individuals to access their innate self-healing mechanism by bypassing the ego. While our ability to contain emotions is a necessary aspect of social development, an over-reliance on this containment can lead to a disconnection from our natural emotional flow and result in long-term health issues.

## Core Concepts

* **The Innate Healing Mechanism**: Like animals and babies, humans naturally possess the ability to express and process sensory information and emotions as they arise, without self-consciousness or restraint.
* **The Role of Containment**: As we mature, learning to contain our emotions is functional and necessary for social settings (e.g., in a professional environment). However, society has taken this containment too far.
* **The Cost of Suppression**: Excessive suppression of emotions creates physical holding patterns and energetic blockages. If these blockages are left unprocessed, they can manifest as chronic physical and psychological conditions over time.
* **Re-establishing the Flow**: Emotional Release acts as a direct line of communication with the emotional body. It facilitates the deep, self-organizing mechanism that inherently wants to restore wholeness.
* **Rapid Transformation**: By removing the barriers erected by the ego and societal conditioning, Emotional Release can create radical, rapid, and positive changes in a person's life and well-being.
