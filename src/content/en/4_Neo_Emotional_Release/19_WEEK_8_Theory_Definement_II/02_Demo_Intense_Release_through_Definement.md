# Demo | Intense Release through Definement

## Key Concepts
- **Somatic Grounding:** Starting with deep breaths, spinal alignment, and tuning into the present moment without any expectations.
- **Locating Resistance:** Bringing awareness to a recurring uncomfortable situation or feeling, and identifying where it manifests physically in the body.
- **Defining the Emotion:** Exploring the physical sensation and identifying the core emotion behind it (e.g., sadness, anger, fear, guilt) along with its physical characteristics like color, shape, or smell.
- **Externalizing and Supporting:** Visualizing the emotion moving into one hand to feel its weight and needs, while using the other hand to offer support, protection, or release.

## Summary
In this guided exercise, the practitioner leads a somatic meditation focused on identifying and defining emotional blockages. The session begins with deep breathing and spinal alignment, encouraging participants to simply be present and drop any expectations. Once grounded, participants are asked to focus on a recurring, uncomfortable issue in their lives and locate where the corresponding resistance or sensation lives in their physical body. 

By placing their hands on the affected area, participants are guided to give the sensation space to grow and express itself. They are encouraged to define the feeling by naming the underlying emotion—whether it is sadness, anger, fear, or even joy—and to explore its sensory details, such as color, shape, or associated memories. This act of definement helps to bring subconscious emotional patterns to the surface.

Finally, the exercise involves a powerful visualization technique where the defined emotion is channeled into one hand. Participants feel its weight and decide what it needs—whether it must be held, protected, or let go. The other hand is then brought in to provide the necessary support, creating a tangible, bodily experience of nurturing and resolving the emotional block.
