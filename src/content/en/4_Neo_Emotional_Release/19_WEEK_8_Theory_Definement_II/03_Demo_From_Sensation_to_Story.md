# Demo | From Sensation to Story

## Key Concepts
- Definement involves giving a sensation a name, identity, or story to help process and understand the underlying emotion.
- Combining resources (like safety) with difficult sensations (like fear) creates a safe container that makes the discomfort less threatening.
- Connecting physical sensations to specific life memories helps integrate the emotional experience.
- Reframing anxiety as "positive excitement" or a source of helpful energy allows the body to build trust rather than just experiencing fear.

## Summary
In this demonstration, the facilitator guides the client through the process of "definement"—giving a specific name and story to their physical sensations. The client initially felt fear and discomfort as a "pinching" sensation they wanted to avoid. By introducing physical resourcing and safety, the client was able to create a secure container for their fear, transforming the experience into one of peace and co-regulation.

The facilitator then encourages the client to connect this embodied feeling of safety and support to a real-life story. The client recalls a frightening experience on a scooter where unexpected help arrived, mirroring the current feeling of their fear being held and supported. This connection allows the mind and body to work together, bringing mental understanding into physical awareness. 

Finally, the session explores how anxiety can be reframed into "positive excitement." The client recognizes that the energy behind anxiety is actually there to help and protect them. By witnessing this energy from a place of safety and trust, the client can process the emotion more fully and integrate it into their nervous system.
