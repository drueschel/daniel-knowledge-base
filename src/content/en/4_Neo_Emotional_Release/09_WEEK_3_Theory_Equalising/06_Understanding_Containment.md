# Understanding Containment

The concept of containment is essential in the healing process. Just as physical entities like cells, organs, and skin act as containers, the body functions as an emotional and energetic container built by our awareness, body, and nervous system.

## The Ideal Emotional Container
* Emotions need a "house" to live in that balances space and boundaries.
* **Rigid Container:** A rigid body with tight, contracted tissue acts like a jail cell, preventing emotions from vibrating outwards, being felt, and witnessed.
* **Boundless Container:** An energetic system that is too open, lacking boundaries, allows emotions to scatter everywhere, typically leading to emotional overwhelm.
* The ideal container provides enough space for emotions to exist while maintaining boundaries to hold and contain them safely.

## Slow Feelers vs. Fast Feelers
This spectrum defines how well a person can contain and host their own emotions:
* **Slow Feelers:** Tend to be numb to their emotions. Their container lacks the necessary space for emotions to emerge and process. They struggle to allow emotions to be felt.
* **Fast Feelers:** Lack sufficient boundaries or containment, causing them to be easily overwhelmed by their emotions.
    * They might feel they are constantly experiencing emotions, but they are often overwhelmed by "cover emotions."
    * Because they cannot hold or contain the emotion, they are unable to access the deeper emotions required for true transformation.
