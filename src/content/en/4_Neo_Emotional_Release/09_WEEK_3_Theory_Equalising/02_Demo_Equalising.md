# Demo: Equalising

This session provides a practical demonstration and theoretical breakdown of the "Equalising" technique in Neo-Emotional Release. The core mechanism involves offering a desired positive outcome to bring subconscious resistance to the surface, and then systematically accepting—or "equalising"—whatever resistance arises.

## The Equalising Process
*   **Define the Outcome:** Start by asking the client what they would like as an outcome (e.g., well-being, being healthy, happy, and pain-free).
*   **Offer the Outcome:** Ask the client if they can welcome or allow that positive outcome right now in the present moment.
*   **Observe the Resistance:** Offering a positive outcome that seems unreachable will naturally trigger resistance (e.g., fear of hostility, feelings of unsafety).
*   **Equalise the Resistance:** Ask the client if they can accept or allow the resistance to be there. The goal is to make the resistance "okay" and equal to any other feeling.

## Working with Layers of Resistance
*   **Accepting the Inability to Accept:** If a client struggles to fully allow a feeling, you can equalize that struggle itself. For example: *"Can you 100% allow that you can't 100% allow?"*
*   **Equalising Hatred of Mechanisms:** Clients often resent their own protective mechanisms (like hyper-vigilance or people-pleasing). You can equalise this by asking: *"Can you allow that you hate that part of yourself?"*
*   **Creating Safety:** By systematically validating every layer of resistance, fear, or frustration, you create a profound sense of safety and calm in the body.

## Key Takeaway
You bring the "light" (the positive outcome) close to bring the "resistance" close. Once the resistance is visible, you don't fight it; you equalise it by making it completely acceptable to feel that way.
