# What Is Emotional Release?

Emotional release is a facilitation process that empowers individuals to access their innate self-healing mechanism—a natural ability often obscured by the ego. By reconnecting with the emotional body, this process allows deep, self-organizing healing to take place, leading to radical and rapid positive changes in a person's life.

## The Natural State of Expression
- **Innate Release:** Animals and babies naturally express sensory information and emotions as they arise, without self-consciousness or concern for external judgment. The emotion flows in and out freely.
- **The Need for Containment:** As we mature, learning to contain our emotional energy is a necessary social adaptation. Complete, unfiltered emotional expression in professional or social settings (like a board meeting) would be chaotic and dysfunctional.

## The Consequences of Over-Containment
- **Emotional Suppression:** While containment is useful, modern society has taken it too far, causing a severe disconnect from our natural ability to release and process energy freely.
- **Physical Impact:** Suppressing emotions creates physical holding patterns and energetic blockages within the system.
- **Chronic Conditions:** When these suppressed emotions and blockages are left unprocessed over time, they manifest as various chronic physical and psychological conditions.

## The Healing Process
- **Direct Communication:** Emotional release provides a direct pathway to communicate with the emotional body.
- **Restoring Wholeness:** It activates the deep self-organizing mechanism that inherently wants to return the body and mind to a state of wholeness, facilitating profound healing.
