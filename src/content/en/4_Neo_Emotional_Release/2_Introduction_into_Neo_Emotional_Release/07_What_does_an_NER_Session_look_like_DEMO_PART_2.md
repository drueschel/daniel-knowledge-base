# Somatic Evolution: What Does an NER Session Look Like?

In a Neo-Emotional Release (NER) session, the goal is to move beyond familiar but limiting emotional states and activate suppressed life force energy. This process involves navigating through resistance, transforming the victim narrative into personal power, and providing dynamic support tailored to the body's subtle cues. 

## Moving Beyond the "Childhood Room"
Many individuals hold onto feelings of helplessness or sadness because they feel familiar and safe, much like a childhood bedroom. A key aspect of a somatic session is recognizing when an emotion is being used to hide from a more difficult, empowering feeling, such as anger or assertiveness. The true release occurs not by dwelling in sadness, but by stepping out of that metaphorical room to reclaim one's voice and power.

## Tracking Somatic Signs
Trapped emotions express themselves through physical sensations. A practitioner must closely observe the body's reactions:
- **Knees and Legs:** Often relate to feelings of stability, grounding, and feeling strong enough to stand up for oneself.
- **Hands:** Stiffness in the hands can indicate holding onto old patterns, being bound by responsibility, or an inability to let go.
- **Shoulders and Traps:** Pushing gently on the shoulders can help disperse the energy tied to burdensome responsibilities, shifting awareness back into the body.
- **The Face:** Shame and self-image are deeply connected to the face. As suppressed energy begins to move, facial muscles often start warming up and twitching.

## The Dance of Father and Mother Energies
An effective therapist intuitively balances two distinct archetypal energies:
- **Father Energy (Empowerment):** Pushing the client to activate their inner strength. When the ego says "you can't" because being powerful feels threatening, the practitioner challenges this by encouraging vocal expression (e.g., "I am [Name] and I can decide"). This activates the inner "yes" and breaks the victim cycle.
- **Mother Energy (Nurturing):** Allowing the client to feel small, safe, and held. After activating their power, the client may need to surrender into vulnerability. Embodying the mother archetype means providing a safe container where the client knows they do not have to pretend to be strong all the time.

## The Result: Coming Home to the Body
When a client successfully channels these energies, the result is a profound sense of feeling alive and "at home" within themselves. The physical shifts—such as changes in skin color, relaxed muscles, and deeper breathing—are natural indicators that old energetic patterns have been released, making way for true emotional freedom.
