# Elements of Release

Neo-Emotional Release (NER) utilizes a powerful combination of methods to facilitate healing by communicating directly with the consciousness behind our emotions. While other therapies may focus solely on the physical dimension, NER addresses the underlying emotional blockages that cause misalignment in the body and mind.

## The Three Core Elements of Release

NER integrates three distinct elements, allowing practitioners to work effectively with almost any individual:

*   **Touch:** Applying the correct pressure to specific "doorways" or areas in the body confronts the stored emotional charge, triggering a release.
*   **Breath:** Intentional breathwork increases the body's capacity to process and integrate emotions.
*   **Verbal Guidance:** Saying the precise right thing at the right time acts like a verbal acupuncture needle. Hitting the right spot mentally and emotionally can initiate profound releases.

## Addressing the Root Cause

While physical therapies (like osteopathy or massage) manipulate the body to correct physical misalignments, they often only indirectly affect the psyche. If the underlying emotional contraction causing the physical tension isn't addressed, true healing is incomplete. NER steps in to communicate directly with these deeper emotional causes, resolving the root of dis-ease and negativity.
