# ► Week 7 Practice Room | Definement

## Key Concepts
- **Definement Technique:** Deepening the emotional process by correctly identifying and naming the present emotion.
- **Brain Synchronization:** When the neocortex (cognition) understands what the limbic system (emotions) is experiencing, the brain and body synchronize.
- **Releasing Repression:** Properly labeling an emotion (e.g., recognizing it as anger rather than fear) allows it to surface rapidly from repression.
- **Deepening the Process:** Asking specific questions (like identifying who the emotion is connected to) provides more clarity, intensifying and facilitating the release.
- **Transforming Energy:** Once resistance stops and the emotion is fully allowed, its energy can feel vital, alive, or even "delicious."

## Summary
The "Definement" technique is a powerful tool used to deepen a client's emotional process by helping them accurately identify the emotion they are experiencing. Because emotions are processed in the limbic system while cognition and thoughts occur in the neocortex, creating a bridge between the two is crucial. When a facilitator asks questions to pinpoint the exact feeling—such as asking if it is fear or anger—the moment the correct emotion is named, the brain and body synchronize, allowing the repressed emotion to surface fully.

In practice, definement involves simple, direct questioning. By asking, "Is it anger?" or "Is this from your mother?", the client's neocortex finally understands the somatic experience. This cognitive recognition causes a profound shift where the emotion blossoms, providing an opportunity for deep release. As demonstrated in the clip, once the emotion (anger) is acknowledged and the resistance to it drops, the raw energy behind it can be experienced as something very vibrant, juicy, and alive, transforming a previously uncomfortable sensation into an empowering state.
