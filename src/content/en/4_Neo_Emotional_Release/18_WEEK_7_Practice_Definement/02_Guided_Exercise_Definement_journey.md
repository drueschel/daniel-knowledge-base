# Guided Exercise | Definement journey

## Key Concepts
- **Definement beyond the mental:** Definement is a holistic process that involves knowing oneself on all levels, including physical expression, vocalization, visualization, and breathing patterns.
- **The role of resonance:** Resonance acts as a guiding compass in finding the correct definition, manifesting as positive (excitement), negative (defensiveness or triggers), or neutral responses. 
- **Opening the door to conversion:** Definement serves as the foundational step in emotional release work, establishing the necessary clarity to transition into deeper processes like conversions.
- **Navigating resistance:** Clients may loop in intellectualized responses out of frustration. Identifying and defining this underlying frustration helps rebuild trust and uncover deeper root causes.
- **Following the life force:** Facilitators use the flow of energy, excitement, and alignment to guide sessions, especially when the initial direction is unclear.

## Summary

Definement is the vital process of identifying and clearly expressing what is occurring within a client's system. Far from being purely intellectual, it requires engaging with all aspects of the self—whether through movement, sound, visualization, or emotional embodiment. This deep self-exploration often involves navigating the unknown or confronting emotions that have been pushed aside. By finding a cohesive definition across all sensory channels, life force is consolidated into a single direction, providing the clarity and motivation needed to address the underlying issue.

The most effective tool for navigating definement is resonance. Facilitators must closely monitor the client's energetic responses to various prompts or reframes. Positive resonance brings excitement and an immediate sense of alignment, while negative resonance—such as defensiveness, laughter, or anger—can equally highlight important triggers that demand definition. When clients exhibit intellectual looping or resistance, expressing that they "already know" the issue, it often masks a deeper frustration or loss of trust. Defining this very frustration becomes the key to breaking the loop. 

Ultimately, definement lays the groundwork for deeper emotional release work, preparing the client for subsequent conversion techniques. Facilitators, having navigated their own internal definement, act as mirrors, helping clients map out possibilities and guiding them toward energetic alignment. By consistently following the flow of life force and resonance, both facilitator and client can move beyond the surface and connect with the profound depths of the emotional experience.
