# Körperschmerzen beim Autonomietyp

## 1. Core Theme & Abstract
For the autonomy type, physical pain is an attempt to feel oneself after losing contact with one's emotions, serving as an indirect expression of repressed grief. Because this type tends to completely deny their own feelings and needs by retreating into their thoughts, pain acts as a substitute mechanism.

## 2. Key Insights
* The autonomy type denies their own feelings and needs, escaping into mental spaces.
* This dynamic naturally creates immense distance from others.
* They project onto others the image of being overpowering yet internally frozen.
* Healing strictly requires direct, safe contact with another person, such as a friend or therapist.

## 3. Quotes
* "This means the autonomy type has completely lost contact with themselves, retreats into the mind... and no longer feels themselves."
* "So that the system can feel itself in any way at all, it creates physical pain."
* "It needs a counterpart, it needs contact. This process cannot be resolved any other way."
