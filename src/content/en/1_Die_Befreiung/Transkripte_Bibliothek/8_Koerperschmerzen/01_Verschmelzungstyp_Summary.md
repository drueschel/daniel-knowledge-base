# Körperschmerzen beim Verschmelzungstyp

## 1. Core Theme & Abstract
The core themes of body pain include unbearableness, being at the mercy of something, and helplessness. For the fusion type, body pain often serves as an indirect means to receive lacking emotional attention on a secondary level by channeling repressed anger and hatred into physical pain.

## 2. Key Insights
* Body pain can be a physical channeling of unconscious anger and hatred.
* The pain acts as a mechanism to indirectly receive care and attention (e.g., from doctors).
* The fusion type creates this dynamic by over-caring for and infantilizing others.
* Behind the symptom lies the fear of being abandoned if they step into their own power.
* To justify their behavior, the fusion type projects weakness and helplessness onto others.

## 3. Quotes
* "With pain, it is very important, as always, to have everything checked by a doctor and/or psychotherapist."
* "Pain is usually very focused on one point."
* "...on the relationship level, this means that I channel the hatred I have inside me, due to a lack of emotional attention from my partner [...], onto a secondary level to indirectly get this attention."
* "The fusion type does this by caring for others, by infantilizing others. They don't actually want that, which is how anger and hatred arise in the background..."
