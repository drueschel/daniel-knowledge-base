# What this is about

The core approach is based on an understanding of **developmental and Attachment Trauma**.

## Focus on the attachment level instead of symptoms
*   **Turning away from symptoms and the past:** The healing path focuses neither on the specific symptom nor on the detailed processing of the past.
*   **The central role of the binding level:** The key lies exclusively on the binding level. Today, old attachment patterns only exist as projections in the head.

## The Path to Full Contact (Reality Check)
*   **Recognizing and comparing:** Your own mental processes (projections from childhood) are determined and specifically compared with today's reality (reality check).
*   **Complete Contact as a Goal:** The nervous system craves complete, safe contact. Only when identification with old projections ends and real contact with the here and now occurs can the nervous system regulate itself.
*   **A new perception of the world:** Current reality is fundamentally different from the threatening world of childhood. Free life comes from this actual contact.
