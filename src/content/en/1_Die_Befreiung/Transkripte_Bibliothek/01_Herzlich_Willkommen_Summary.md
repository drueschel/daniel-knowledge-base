# 01 Welcome

Problems and situations can be translated into the attachment context in order to identify and address deeper causes at the relationship level that have remained undetected in previous attempts at solutions.

## Core concepts and goals

- **Focus on Attachment Patterns:** Many long-lasting and seemingly insoluble problems arise because the root cause lies deep in early attachment patterns and these have not yet been addressed or updated.
- **From problem to solution:** Systematic translation of challenges into the attachment context to get to the true cause at the relationship level.
- **Regaining the ability to act:** By understanding the deep connections, the power to shape and act is regained and the feeling of being at the mercy is dissolved.
- **Area of ​​application:** The method is suitable for both personal self-analysis and for therapists when working with clients.
