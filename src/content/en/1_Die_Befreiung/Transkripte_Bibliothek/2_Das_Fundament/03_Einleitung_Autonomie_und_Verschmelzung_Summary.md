# Autonomy and fusion

The goal is to recognize your own nervous system type (autonomy or fusion) in order to better understand automatic reactions and patterns.

## Recognizing your own type: The first impulse

To find out in which direction you are leaning, the very first involuntary impulse that arises **before** thinking begins is crucial.

*   **Merging Type:** The first impulse when meeting people is a forward movement. The urge to make contact and communicate arises.
*   **Autonomy Type:** The first impulse is to move away. The immediate need arises to create distance and avoid contact ("Let's get away").

> [!TIP]
> **Exercise / reflection question:** Imagine you are walking through the city and meeting people. What is your very first impulse that kicks in immediately? Do you want to get in touch or distance yourself?

## Countermovements and compensation (fantasy vs. reality)

Both types often develop internal countermoves and fantasies that can lead to confusion about their own true type. However, the decisive factor for classification is not the inner longing, but the actual, lived reality.

*   **The Illusion of the Merging Type:** Because this type is stuck in a constant movement toward others, it can exhaust them over time. He then often develops fantasies of complete independence (e.g. traveling around the world alone, living completely self-sufficiently). However, the factual reality of life is that he is constantly busy with people and looking for contact.
*   **The illusion of the Autonomy Type:** This type actually lives in withdrawal, isolates himself and avoids contact. The unconscious need for contact or the resulting feeling of loneliness may cause one to dream of states of fusion (e.g. experiencing oneness with the universe or being anesthetized by substances to create an artificial state of oneness).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Reality Check:** Look at what your life *actually* looks like now, not what you want or how you're internally trying to compensate for things. Are you constantly involved in activities with other people (fusion) or do you live in withdrawal (autonomy)?

## Dealing with inner resistance

*   **Resistance is a positive sign:** If information triggers inner resistance, it means that the content reaches the depths and sets something in motion psychologically.
*   **Don't fight:** Don't try to fight this resistance. Just take him with you benevolently. It is not necessary to immediately rationally understand or completely accept everything.
