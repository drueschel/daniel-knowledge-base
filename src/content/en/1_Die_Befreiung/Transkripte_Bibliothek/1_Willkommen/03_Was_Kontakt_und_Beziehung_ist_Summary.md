# 04 What contact and relationship is

There is a fundamental difference between real, nourishing contact and everyday "pseudo-contact". Despite supposed relationships, many people feel isolated and unhappy because the deep exchange of information that is essential for the survival of the nervous system is missing. This direct contact is necessary in order to experience security and regulation in one's own organism without having to change or go through strenuous processes.

## The difference between real contact and pseudo contact

*   **Pseudo-contact (The social norm):**
    *   Exchange of trivialities (weather, plans, superficial thoughts).
    *   Avoiding direct communication about your own feelings, especially in relation to the physical counterpart.
    *   **Consequence:** The organism does not feel safe, the nervous system cannot relax, and despite apparent social integration, symptoms or problems arise on the outside.
*   **Real Contact (The Nourishing Exchange of Information):**
    *   honest sharing of what's really going on inside yourself (e.g. "I feel sadness," "I feel anger," or "My head thinks you might want to leave me").
    *   Mutual attentive listening when the other person shares his or her true experience.
    *   **Consequence:** If this exchange takes place over a longer period of time, the natural side effect is a deep, real relationship. This direct contact leads to co-regulation and relaxation of the nervous system.

## Healing through pure sharing

*   **No self-optimization necessary:** There is no need to change yourself, to “heal away” something, or to go through difficult, cathartic processes.
*   **Communicate boundaries instead of overcoming them:** Even barriers, fears or resistance do not have to be overcome. It is sufficient to simply and honestly communicate the current status – including existing boundaries.
*   **Security through authenticity:** The longed-for connection and relaxation arise all by themselves by verbally expressing what is already present in your own system at that moment.
