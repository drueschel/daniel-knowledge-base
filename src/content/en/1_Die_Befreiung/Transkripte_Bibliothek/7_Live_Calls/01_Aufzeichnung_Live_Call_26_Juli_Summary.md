# Live Call: July 26th (Lack of Funds)

## 1. Initial situation & symptom
A lifelong, stressful feeling of **lack of money** (having to "chase" money) as well as associated emotions (injustice, anger, fear, sadness) are often based on an underlying relationship issue rather than on external financial causes.

## 2. Identified binding type & core complex
At the relationship level, the feeling of lack is reflected in the impression of not receiving enough care, attention or genuine interest.

- **Core complex (inner belief):** *"I have no value, I have to achieve something in order not to be abandoned."*
- **Projection:** Other people are perceived as weak, who need help and would leave immediately if performance or added value is not consistently provided.

## 3. Therapeutic approach & key questions
Shifting focus from the external symptom (money) to the internal bonding dynamics in the **here and now** by directly mirroring behavior that creates distance.

- **Central awareness:** Compensation for emotional distress (loneliness) through constant "doing", "doing" and cross-border questions.
- **Core questions for reality testing:**
  - *"What threatens me in your imagination?"*
  - *"Are you planning to leave me?"*
  - *"Do you need something from me?"*
- **Demonstration:** The assumption *"The other person will go away if I don't do anything"* is a childhood illusion that does not apply in the current real situation.

## 4. Defense mechanisms in contact
Strong defense mechanisms prevent real, uncontrolled contact:

- **Escape through control:** Continuous questioning, analyzing and attempts to "manage" the conversation subconsciously serve as a protective mechanism to maintain dominance and avoid one's own neediness.
- **Mental exit (dissociation):** When confronted with reality, the mind switches to "emptiness" ("emergency shutdown", "plug pulled") or flees into concepts of the future and past.
- **Intellectualization:** Strategic explanations instead of sharing deep feelings interrupt actual contact.

## 5. Therapeutic result & findings
Through persistent presence and direct checking of projections in contact, the defensive construct collapses:

- **Realization of Illusion:** Suffering is based entirely on an old, untrue thought ("I will be abandoned if I do nothing"*).
- **Physical Relaxation:** Inner "leaning back" and release of pressure occurs as soon as the struggle and control are given up.
- **Self-empowerment:** Once the illusion is seen through, the inner (and outer) lack loses its power. Space is created for allowing deeper feelings (e.g. despair), which paradoxically leads to calming.
