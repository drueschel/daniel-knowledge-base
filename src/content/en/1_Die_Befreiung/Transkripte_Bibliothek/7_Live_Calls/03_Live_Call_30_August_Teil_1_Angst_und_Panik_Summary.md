# Live Call: August 30 – Part 1 (Anxiety & Panic)

> 📜 **Full Verbatim Transcript (59:47 Min.):** [Read the full transcript of this call](./Volltranskripte/03_Live_Call_30_August_Teil_1_Angst_und_Panik_Volltranskript)

## 1. Initial Situation & Symptom
The client has suffered for many years from severe **agoraphobia** and panic attacks that drastically restrict her physical radius of movement (acute shortness of breath and cold sweats arise after just three traffic lights or leaving her neighborhood). Earlier symptoms (such as panic in supermarket checkout queues) were managed via avoidance strategies, but the symptom continually shifted to new targets.
- **Purpose of Symptom:** Panic acts as a protective barrier (a "rubber band") preventing departure into the world in order to avoid confronting the core terror of relationship loss and rejection.

## 2. Identified Attachment Style & Core Complex
- **Attachment Style:** Merger Type (Anxious-Preoccupied) with severe trauma related to abandonment.
- **Unconscious Causal Chain (Projection):**
  - *Eye movement / gaze of the therapist* $\rightarrow$ Interpreted as: *"He is impatient and annoyed with me."*
  - *Being annoyed* $\rightarrow$ Interpreted as: *"When someone is annoyed, they leave / terminate the relationship."*
  - *Being abandoned* $\rightarrow$ Existential annihilation / childhood life threat.
- **Core Complex:** A harmless visual sensory cue is instantaneously inflated into a childhood catastrophe fantasy without any sensory reality check.

## 3. Therapeutic Procedure & Key Inquiries
Shifting focus entirely away from external symptoms (driving, traffic lights, queues) toward direct connection in the **Here and Now**:

- **Unmasking the Dream State (Childhood Trance):**
  Gopal directly reflects that the client is caught in an old childhood dream that has nothing to do with present reality:
  - *"You are dreaming. You are recreating your childhood. Random sensory impressions with zero meaning become: 'He is annoyed, he will leave in a second.'"*
- **Direct Reality Testing (Inquiry in Contact):**
  - *"Does it mean you are annoyed and impatient because of your eye movement?"*
  - **Clarification by Gopal:** *"No, absolutely not! I look bottom-right because my clock is there, and to the right because your gallery view is there. It has nothing to do with being annoyed. I am sitting here relaxed and enjoy working with you."*
- **Dismantling the False Dichotomy:**
  Gopal illustrates that even when an adult feels annoyed, there are countless constructive options (naming feelings, eating chocolate, setting boundaries) rather than abruptly terminating the relationship.
- **Live In-Session Behavioral Experiment:**
  Gopal invites the client to physically stand up, walk through her apartment, and return to verify with her own senses whether the therapist is still present:
  - *Result:* The therapist is still there — the ancient abandonment script collapses upon sensory verification.

## 4. Defense Mechanisms in Contact
- **Distrust & Invalidation of Reality:** Even after the projection is dismantled, the client asks doubtfully (*"Did you tell me the truth? You could just be saying that to calm me down"*), attempting to preserve the familiar trauma worldview.
- **Symptom Fixation as a Smokescreen:** Clinging to driving and physical distance issues to avoid facing emotional vulnerability and relational neediness.
- **Passivity & Learned Helplessness:** Lingering in childhood helplessness instead of opening the eyes to perceive what is physically present in the outside world.

## 5. Therapeutic Outcome & Insights
- **Symptom Collapse Through Genuine Contact:** As soon as the client perceives Gopal in the present moment and recognizes that neither abandonment nor threat exists, the phobia of leaving home instantly loses its relevance.
- **The Core Law of Attachment Anxiety:**
  - *"The more you detach from people through your projections, the stronger your anxieties will grow."*
  - *"The more you step into authentic contact and verify what is actually real, the more you awaken from the childhood nightmare."*
- **Somatic Relief:** Pronounced drop in nervous system activation and restoration of visual clarity once internal resistance against reality ceases.
