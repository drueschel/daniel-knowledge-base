# Live Call: October 04 (Anxiety & Panic)

> 📜 **Full Verbatim Transcript (55:48 Min.):** [Read the full transcript of this call](./Volltranskripte/05_Live_Call_04_Oktober_Angst_und_Panik_Volltranskript)

## 1. Initial Situation & Symptom
The client is in a chronic state of **acute panic, racing thoughts, and terror of impending death**. He experiences constant internal sirens, feeling in immediate peril of dying, and projecting danger onto virtually all external surroundings. Additionally, magical thinking is present, fearing his own thoughts could cause physical harm to others.
- **Purpose of Symptom:** Hyperfixation on the internal horror script absorbs the nervous system's capacity, preventing confrontation with underlying early childhood helplessness and abandonment.

## 2. Identified Attachment Style & Core Complex
- **Attachment Style:** Merger Type / Freeze State (Sympathetic hyperarousal loop).
- **Core Complex:** Total trauma flooding from early childhood:
  - *"I am in mortal danger, no one is protecting me, I am dying right now."*
- **Dissociative Trance:** The mind is possessed by catastrophic internal imagery, completely decoupled from external sensory input.

## 3. Therapeutic Procedure & Key Inquiries
Radical interruption of obsessive ruminations and systematic sensory re-anchoring in the **Here and Now**:

- **No Psychoanalysis During Panic Flooding:**
  Gopal halts all cognitive analysis of fears:
  - *"There is nothing to figure out! You only need to open your eyes and look outward."*
- **Sensory Grounding via Concrete Visual Data:**
  Gopal anchors the client's physiology by querying specific environmental attributes:
  - *"What color are the tiles in front of you?"* $\rightarrow$ *"Brown."*
  - *"What color is the toaster?"* $\rightarrow$ *"Blue plastic."*
  - *"What do you see in the picture on the wall?"* $\rightarrow$ *"Trees with sunlight."*
- **The Neurobiological Law of Movement vs. Rest:**
  - *"Under mortal threat, organisms must move (fight or flight). If nothing in the room is moving and no one is attacking you, there is no threat — and the organism rests."*
- **Reality Testing in Connection:**
  - *"Is any threat coming from me? Am I doing anything to you? No, I am here to help you."*
  - *"Do you realize that you are not dying right now? Your body is alive."*
  - *"You hold the power today: You can choose whether to spiral inward, or engage with reality out there."*

## 4. Defense Mechanisms in Contact
- **Addiction to the Internal Horror Loop:** Repeatedly attempting to drag the dialogue back to internal fear sensations rather than looking outward.
- **Anticipatory Control:** The belief *"I must anticipate and know danger in advance to save myself"* acts as an illusion that fuels panic.
- **Visual Tunnel Vision:** In high panic, the visual field narrows and disengages, enabling projections to dominate unchallenged.

## 5. Therapeutic Outcome & Insights
- **Immediate Somatic Regulation via External Perception:**
  As the client actively observes the physical items and Gopal's presence, physiological down-regulation occurs:
  - *"I can actually see better now... Contours are sharper... My body is suddenly calm."*
- **The Fundamental Truth:**
  - *"There is no link between your internal panic states and external physical reality. You are no longer in mortal danger. It is over."*
- **Stabilization Precedes Deep Process Work:**
  During acute trauma flooding, cognitive or emotional processing fails. The nervous system must first be grounded via the five physical senses in the present room before relational contact can occur.
