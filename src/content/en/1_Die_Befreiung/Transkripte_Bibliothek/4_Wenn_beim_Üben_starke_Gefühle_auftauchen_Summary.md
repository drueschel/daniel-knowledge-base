# When strong feelings arise while practicing

If strong emotions or intense states are activated during the course or exercises, this is generally a positive sign that deeper psychological levels are being touched. However, it is essential not to overwhelm yourself, but rather to maintain your own capacity limits and use active self-regulation when necessary.

## Active self-regulation vs. repression
* **Allowing without intervening:** If feelings arise through the content or exercises, just try to notice them and let them be there without actively wanting to do anything with them.
* **Recognize capacities:** Our ability to integrate emotions and intense states has limits. The organism has to slowly get used to certain emotional depths.
* **Conscious termination:** Check again and again: *Is this state still tenable for me? Do I still feel comfortable and safe with this?* If the answer is no or external circumstances demand attention, stop the exercise and ensure that the intensity decreases.
* **Building stability:** Consciously stopping a process when you are overwhelmed is not a repression mechanism, but rather active self-regulation. If this is practiced consistently, the necessary stability is created in the nervous system in order to be able to allow and integrate more and more in the long term.

## Boundaries as the foundation of transformation
* **Get rid of social conditioning:** We are often conditioned to ignore our needs in order to function further and faster. For real emotional work, this pattern must be reversed.
* **Respect for your own boundaries:** Strictly respecting your own boundaries is the absolute foundation of any sustainable transformation. If these are ignored, no stable basis can be created.
* **Faster Progress:** Paradoxically, the personal process is much faster and deeper when one's boundaries are maintained and respected at all times.
