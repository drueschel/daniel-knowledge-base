# Konflikte beim Autonomietyp

## 1. Core Theme & Abstract
This transcript sheds light on how the autonomy type generates conflicts in romantic relationships. Although the basic pattern is similar to that of the fusion type (arguments and disputes), this occurs through complete withdrawal, abdication of responsibility, and an escape into abstract realms of thought. This overloads the partner, thereby triggering anger and conflict.

## 2. Key Insights
* The autonomy type unconsciously denies their own existence, along with their needs and feelings.
* By transferring all burdens to the counterpart, conflict is indirectly provoked.
* Overcoming this dynamic requires bringing to awareness and questioning one's own projections regarding the partner.

## 3. Quotes
* "And that means, for the partner, there suddenly arises an excess of responsibility, burden, and having to care. And that logically makes the other angry, and that is how conflicts arise."
* "Exactly by pretending that they don't even exist, they deny their entire existence, their feelings and needs, and simply keep themselves away from the partner."
* "[...] by completely shifting the focus away from themselves and their counterpart into pure thinking and abstract contexts."
