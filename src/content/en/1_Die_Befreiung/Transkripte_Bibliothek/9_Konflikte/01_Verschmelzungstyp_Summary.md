# Conflicts in the Fusion Type

## 1. Core Theme & Abstract
This text addresses relationship conflicts and specifically explores how the "fusion type" (Verschmelzungstyp) unconsciously uses them. It first categorizes conflicts into three levels: harmless arguments over projections, genuinely destructive behavior, and real physical danger. For the fusion type, conflicts and arguments serve as an unconscious substitute for genuine closeness, as expressing true needs directly is hindered by misplaced pride and an inability to be vulnerable.

## 2. Key Insights
* **Three Levels of Conflict:** (1) Harmless projections (e.g., arguments about household chores), (2) destructive behavior without direct danger (e.g., infidelity or verbal outbursts), and (3) real physical danger (e.g., violence, addiction) which requires professional help.
* **Conflict as a Substitute for Closeness:** The fusion type uses fighting and arguments as an avoidance strategy because they are incapable of true intimacy and direct emotional exchange.
* **Fear of Vulnerability:** Out of misplaced pride, their own genuine needs and feelings are not communicated directly in the relationship.
* **The Fusion Type's Pattern:** They create conflict by infantilizing their partner (treating them as helpless or dependent), over-caretaking, and simultaneously ignoring or bypassing the partner's actual needs.

## 3. Quotes
* "The first level or the first version is, so to speak, the most harmless; this is what is covered in this course. Problems, conflicts related to illusory projections."
* "...I am too proud to express my needs and feelings directly and create arguments and disputes as a substitute for [closeness and] intimacy."
* "Ultimately, this means I create the conflict because I am incapable of intimacy and genuine exchange."
* "The fusion type does this by infantilizing others, taking care of them, and acting as if they were helpless and dependent..."
