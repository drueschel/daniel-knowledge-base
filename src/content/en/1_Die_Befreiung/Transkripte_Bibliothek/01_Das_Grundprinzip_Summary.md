# 01: The basic principle

## 1. Core topic & abstract
The foundation of the work: **There is nothing wrong with you.**
The goal of therapeutic work is not self-optimization, repairing something "broken" or changing one's own personality. It's all about a **change of perspective**.
The current suffering (symptoms, problems, circumstances) arises from a distortion of perception, which was established in childhood as a survival mechanism. Returning to reality reveals that in reality there was never a problem with one's existence.

## 2. The most important insights
* **No self-optimization:** It is neither necessary nor beneficial to try to rebuild or improve yourself. The focus is on the way of looking at it, not on the object of the view.
* **The Prism Metaphor:** A healthy nervous system is like a pure prism that refracts light naturally. Traumatic experiences in childhood act like impacts into this prism - the light (perception) is distorted, constructing bizarre and painful realities.
* **Symptoms as guides:** External problems (e.g. lack of money, burnout) are not the actual problem. They are the compass that points to the unresolved attachment conflict.
* **From the outside to the inside:** The method gradually directs the focus from the superficial symptom, via the interpersonal relationship constellation, to the deepest thought patterns in the bonding context.

## 3. Quotes
> *"This is not about fundamentally rebuilding, bending, changing, improving, optimizing something in you. That doesn't happen here at all and it's not necessary."*

> *"You don't suffer from the symptom in principle, but from the attachment conflict."*

> *"If you go along with it and get involved with the reality as it is today, then you will see that in reality you have always been okay and that there has never been a problem with you in reality."*
