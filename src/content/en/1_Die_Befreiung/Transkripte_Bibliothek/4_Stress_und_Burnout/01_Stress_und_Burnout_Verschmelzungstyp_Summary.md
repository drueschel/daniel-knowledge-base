# Stress & Burnout – Merging Type

Stress and burnout are often not just the results of external stress, but rather symptoms of a deeply anchored attachment pattern. For the Merging Type, this means completely sacrificing oneself in relationships and in life in order to subconsciously avoid being abandoned.

## The internal dynamics of the Merging Type

* **Sacrifice as a survival strategy:** The Merging Type literally works themselves to death for others, in the constant hope that their own needs will finally be seen.
* **Infantilization of others:** Today's relationship constellation is created by infantilizing others and treating them like little children or mothering them. At the same time, help and support from others is unconsciously rejected.
* **Protection against abandonment:** This self-sacrificing behavior serves as a protective strategy to prevent the deepest fear from occurring: **abandonment**.

## Core beliefs and deepest emotions

* **The Toxic Belief:** The Merging Type deeply believes that their own autonomy, freedom, and expression of their own truth will inevitably cause the other person to leave.
* **Repressed anger beneath the grief:** Beneath a superficial layer of loneliness, sadness and despair, at the deepest level lies **repressed anger and hatred** about this constant, draining adjustment process.
* **Projection onto others:** In order to maintain this construct, the Merging Type projects onto his counterpart that he is small, weak and dependent on him.

## The Reality Check: The Key Questions

In order to dissolve these deeply anchored beliefs, it is not enough to just treat the stress symptom. Internal beliefs about attachment and relationships need to be aligned with reality.

**Exercise for the Merging Type:** Go to someone close to you and ask the following questions (reality check) to check your attachment pattern:

1. **“Is it true that you will leave if I separate myself and be independent?”**
2. **"Do you have a problem with me telling you that I feel anger?"**
3. **"Is it true that you feel small, weak and dependent on me?"**

After that, it's all about **really listening** to the answer in order to overwrite the old attachment pattern with the actual reality in the here and now.
