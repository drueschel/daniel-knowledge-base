# Individual Session: Money Scarcity – Part 2 (Avoidance & Fear of Intimacy)

> 📜 **Full Verbatim Transcript (approx. 33 Min.):** [Read the full transcript of this session](./Volltranskripte/08_Einzelsitzung_Geldmangel_Teil_2_Volltranskript)

## 1. Initial Situation & Symptom
The client reports substantial debt, an overdrawn bank account, and severe behavioral paralysis: he stays home, avoids working, and isolates himself socially.
- **Purpose of Symptom:** By remaining passive within his four walls, the client broadcasts an unconscious message to the outside world: *"Leave me alone."* The financial predicament and debt act as an external buffer preventing intimate relationships from developing.

## 2. Identified Attachment Style & Core Complex
- **Attachment Style:** Autonomy Type (Avoidant-Dismissive) with intense shame regarding personal vulnerability and neediness.
- **Core Complex:**
  - *"If women or other people see that I am in distress, helpless, or weak — or that I have a genuine desire for closeness —, they will all run away in disgust."*
- **Projection:**
  - The client is convinced that others are fleeing from him. In reality, **he himself** flees from all interpersonal encounters to prevent experiencing rejection and shame.

## 3. Therapeutic Procedure & Key Inquiries
Gopal dismantles this projection step by step in live contact:

- **Exposing the Cognitive Distortion:**
  Gopal asks: *"If women fled whenever someone desired closeness, you would see women sprinting away from men on every street corner. Do you see that?"* – Client: *"No, of course not. It is only my projection. I am running away from them."*
- **Reality Testing in the Here and Now:**
  Gopal encourages the client to verbalize his forbidden vulnerability directly in the therapeutic setting:
  - *"Gopal, do you want to get away from me right now?"*
    - **Gopal's Answer:** *"No."*
  - *"Gopal, when I tell you that I have a need for closeness, is that a problem for you?"*
    - **Gopal's Answer:** *"No, not at all. It is pleasant to hear that."*
- **Somatic Shift:**
  At this exact juncture, old neural defenses collapse. Genuine, relieving tears of touching and connection emerge, accompanied by physical lightness and profound relief.

## 4. Defense Mechanisms in Contact
- **Social Withdrawal & Isolation:** Staying indoors and refusing work so no one can witness his inner emotional child.
- **Dissociation of Vulnerability:** Interpreting emotional neediness as a mortal flaw.
- **Trauma Transference:** Treating the present adult environment as though everyone will react as cold and dismissive as caregivers did in childhood.

## 5. Therapeutic Outcome & Insights
- **Distinguishing Tears:**
  Gopal reflects that these tears are authentic, healing tears of connection and relief — in contrast to defensive weeping.
- **Liberation from Scarcity:**
  Once sensorially verified that revealing neediness does not repel the counterpart but fosters authentic intimacy, avoidance loses its utility.
- **Outlook:**
  Financial scarcity resolves organically as the client re-enters the world and discovers that human connection is safe and nourishing.
