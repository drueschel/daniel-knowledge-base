# Individual Session: Money Scarcity – Part 1 (Andrea)

> 📜 **Full Verbatim Transcript (approx. 43 Min.):** [Read the full transcript of this session](./Volltranskripte/07_Einzelsitzung_Geldmangel_Teil_1_Volltranskript)

## 1. Initial Situation & Symptom
The client (Andrea) reports chronic financial difficulties and mounting debts. A distinctive behavioral pattern is evident: she obsessively spends money on training courses, seminars, and books because she inwardly feels inadequate, "dumb," and unable to retain knowledge.
- **Purpose of Symptom:** The debt and obsessive intellectual learning serve as an external smokescreen to avoid confronting deep-seated childhood attachment fear and helplessness.

## 2. Identified Attachment Style & Core Complex
- **Attachment Style:** Merger Type (Anxious-Preoccupied) with suppressed autonomy and unexpressed aggression.
- **Unconscious Core Belief:**
  - *"If I enter into real contact, the other person will harm me and leave me."*
  - To ward off the terrifying prospect of abandonment, the client resorts to tears and learned helplessness.
- **Core Complex (Projection):**
  - Beneath apparent sadness lies suppressed rage and hatred regarding childhood boundary violations. Rather than mobilizing this anger as a healthy movement toward autonomy, the client swallows it down and plunges into a regressive crying state.

## 3. Therapeutic Procedure & Key Inquiries
Gopal eliminates the distraction on the content level and firmly directs her attention to immediate relational contact:

- **Halting the Defensive Tears:**
  Gopal firmly interrupts her weeping:
  - *"I suggest you stop crying. These are not genuine tears of grief; this is attachment defense. Tears are for grief or to pacify an overwhelming monster. When anger is swallowed, these tears appear. That is self-destructive."*
- **Unmasking the Projection:**
  - The client projects her own suppressed autonomy impulse onto the other person: *"I don't want to leave — you want to leave! You desire autonomy, but fear it, and then imagine separation in the other person."*
- **Direct Reality Check in Contact:**
  - Gopal instructs her to ask him directly:
    - *"Gopal, do you plan to lie to me or abandon me?"*
    - **Gopal's Answer:** *"No. I have zero reason to do so. I need nothing from you. There is no problem with you."*

## 4. Defense Mechanisms in Contact
- **Retreat into Helplessness & Crying:** Weeping is used unconsciously to pacify the counterpart and avoid authentic, empowered contact.
- **Cognitive Rumination ("Circling"):** The urge to escape into mental concepts, taking notes, and isolating inside her own thoughts rather than looking at and listening to the real person.
- **Anger Avoidance:** Terror that asserting firm boundaries and expressing anger will trigger immediate relational destruction.

## 5. Therapeutic Outcome & Insights
- **Exiting the Mental Carousel:**
  Healing does not happen through more thinking, but through using the physical senses in the here and now: *"Use your eyes to look outside, use your ears to hear what the person is actually saying."*
- **Dissolving the Illusion of Danger:**
  As Andrea realizes that no danger threatens in the present moment with the therapist, her nervous system settles noticeably.
- **Money as a Mirror of Connection:**
  Scarcity arises because nothing is internally given due to fear of being hurt. Once authentic contact is experienced as safe, external financial scarcity naturally dissolves.
