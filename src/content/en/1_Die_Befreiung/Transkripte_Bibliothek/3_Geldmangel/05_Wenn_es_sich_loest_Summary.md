# When it comes off

It is not the result or the answer of the other person that is important, but rather the courage to ask the question. The honest expression of one's own fears and the subsequent exchange are the real beginning of real transformation and true contact - regardless of how the relationship ultimately ends.

## The value of the question
* **50 percent of the transformation** consists solely in having the courage to ask the other person the decisive questions.
* The result or the specific answer is secondary. The act of making contact and expressing the inner reality is the biggest and most important step.

## When your own fears are confirmed
Even if the worst fear occurs and the other person confirms the fear (e.g. with a “yes”), this is not a catastrophe. As an adult, survival is no longer at risk. Rather, it is the first time that you no longer navigate around the inner conflict, but the truth is finally on the table.

### For the merge type
* **Scenario:** You ask your partner if they want to leave you, and your partner confirms that they are thinking about breaking up.
* **Dynamics:** The Merging Type often unconsciously creates an impulse in the partner to want to create more distance.
* **Solution:** This admission is not the end, but the beginning of the real exchange. You can now explore together *why* your partner wants to leave and how the dynamic works. This is the absolute core of the relationship and must not be avoided.

### For the Autonomy Type
* **Scenario:** You ask whether the other person has a problem with your own feelings (such as loneliness or sadness) and you receive a "yes" or the feedback that the other person is currently avoiding the exchange.
* **Dynamics:** Such reactions from the partner have a reason, which often lies in the fact that the Autonomy Type is intangible, withdraws and does not reveal anything about themselves.
* **Solution:** The dialogue now begins here too. By asking why in-depth exchange is difficult, the very contact that has always been avoided since childhood arises.

## Contact is more important than the result
The ultimate goal is to start the exchange and make real contact. Transformation occurs through this honest communication, not through the ultimate outcome of the relationship (even if it leads to separation). When the fear of a "negative" answer disappears, the way becomes clear to analyze further symptoms step by step.
