# Perception and contact

At the beginning of inner work, it is completely normal to experience a phase of inner disorientation in which thoughts, feelings and physical sensations are difficult to distinguish from one another. This initial confusion is not an obstacle, but rather the natural starting point on the path to greater inner clarity and awareness.

## Inner disorientation as a natural starting point
* **Lack of training:** In our culture we are not trained to perceive our internal states (thoughts, feelings, physical sensations) in a clearly differentiated manner.
* **Meaning of the therapeutic work:** The goal of the work is to gradually develop this inner clarity. The initial ambiguity is just the starting point.

## The healing way to deal with confusion
* **Honest acknowledgment of the lack of clarity:** The most important step is to admit your own lack of direction. Instead of trying to cognitively analyze the condition or force clarity, it is more helpful to internally name the ambiguity.
* **Patience is the key:** Clarity develops over time by regularly turning your attention inward.

> **💡 Practical note:**
> When inner confusion occurs and the levels (thoughts, feelings, body) are difficult to separate, one should not attempt to solve the problem cognitively. Instead, the confusion can be clearly stated, for example: *"I'm disoriented right now. I don't know which level is active."* This takes the pressure off and creates space for the process.
