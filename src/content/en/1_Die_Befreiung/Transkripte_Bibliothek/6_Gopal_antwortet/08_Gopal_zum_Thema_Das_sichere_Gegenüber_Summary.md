# Gopal on the topic “The safe counterpart”

For deep psychological transformation and healing, we do not need countless people, nor do we need to reveal our innermost being in every life situation. The central message is: a single, secure relationship is enough. Above all, this process requires security and a counterpart with whom there are no existential dependencies or deep, unresolved family entanglements. If this deeper bond is achieved, the need for constant self-defense and the urge to seek fulfillment elsewhere are significantly reduced.

## The importance of a single caregiver
- **Security as a foundation:** Transformation absolutely requires a safe environment and a deep relationship of trust.
- **One relationship is enough:** It is not necessary - and often even counterproductive - to artificially deepen contact with everyone. A single reference person is completely sufficient for the transformation.
- **Suitable relationship partners:** A life partner, a therapist, a spiritual teacher or a very close, long-term friend are ideal people for this path.
- **Mutual consent:** Both people must have an honest interest in deepening the exchange and practicing conscious cooperation.

## Unsuitable environments and people
- **The public and professional space:** Authorities, the workplace or chance encounters do not provide the necessary safe environment. They are not suitable for this type of opening until the fundamental conflict is resolved.
- **The family of origin:** This is generally not recommended. In the family of origin, old, deeply imprinted and unfavorable patterns are usually active, which kick in very quickly. It often represents an unsafe environment for the process to begin.
- **Lack of interest on the part of the partner:** If your life partner is not interested in this form of exchange, this must be accepted. The task is then to consciously look for someone else to practice together.

## The goal and result of the transformation
Once security is established and transformation is experienced in a single true bond, healing occurs. The constant urge to look outside for fulfillment and the need to constantly protect oneself are continually decreasing.
