# Question Protocol: Reality Check (Merge Type)
*A template for the reality check using the Gopal method*

This protocol is designed specifically for you to go through step by step with a safe counterpart (partner, friend, therapist).

---

## Preparation (The rules of the game for both of you)
Before you start, briefly clarify the general conditions:

*   **For your counterpart:** The only task is to listen to you and answer your questions (in phase 3) **honestly, briefly and without long explanations**. No discussion, no advice, no comforting.
*   **For you:** Your job is to **really let the answers sink in** (listen!).

---

## Phase 1: Recognize the initial situation
The Merging Type often suffers from extreme stress or burnout because they are constantly caring for others and working away. However, he does not do this out of pure charity, but rather as an unconscious **survival strategy**.

The real driver behind this stress is the deep, childlike fear: *"If I don't take care of myself constantly, I will be abandoned."*

---

## Phase 2: Uncover the illusion about the other person
In order to maintain this constant concern, you project a false image onto the other person. You subconsciously believe: *"My partner/opposite is weak, small and completely dependent on my care."*
Only through this illusion does the merger have a right to exist in the relationship.

---

## Phase 3: The Reality Check (The 4 Core Questions)
Healing doesn't happen through talking about the "stress", but rather when the nervous system learns that this childish fear is no longer real today.
Ask your counterpart exactly these four questions, one after the other. Listen to the answers.

**1. Question (fear of loss):**
> *"Are you planning to leave me?"*

**2. Question (Suppressed Autonomy 1):**
> *"Do you have a problem if I say I feel anger?"*

**3. Question (Suppressed Autonomy 2):**
> *"Do you have a problem when I do my thing?"*

**4. Question (Shatter the Projection):**
> *"Is it true that you feel small and dependent on me?"*

---

## Phase 4: The Projection Break (The Key Moment)
If the other person says clearly in question 4: *"No. I don't need any care. I don't need that from you."*, the illusion is shattered.

At this moment the “scene pushing” collapses. You realize (at the nervous system level) that your constant stress and worrying was completely unnecessary to secure the bond. This often means that the true, underlying emotion (loneliness, fear, suppressed anger) becomes noticeable and the unhealthy stress immediately falls away.
