# Lack of money: From the symptom to the level of attachment – ​​Merging Type

The fundamental principle of external lack reflects a deep emotional lack in relationships that is unconsciously self-created to ensure a false form of security.

## From symptom to basic principle

* **The core pattern:** The principle behind lack of money is "lack" - something is missing, there is too little or simply not there.
* **Translation to the attachment level:** A material deficiency in the relationship context means a lack of emotional connection, the absence of a partner, emotional distance or the feeling of not really being heard. The first important step is to admit that the real suffering is a relationship deficiency.

## The unconscious mechanism: distance through the search for proximity

Although the Merging Type seems to continually seek closeness and care for others, their behavior actually creates distance:

* **Excessive caring and infantilization:** Others are often unconsciously viewed as small, weak or helpless and treated accordingly.
* **Avoiding acceptance:** Your own needs are not taken into account in the contact. It is extremely difficult to accept anything from people close to you, as this would jeopardize the image of your own strength and the helplessness of others.
* **Apparent closeness as avoidance of contact:** Constant care is not a real, nourishing relationship on equal terms. It is an avoidance strategy that prevents true intimacy and real closeness.

## Suppressed emotions and projections

* **Security through distance:** The distance created unconsciously serves as protection. The Merging Type constantly tries to avoid being abandoned by the other person.
* **Repressed anger beneath grief:** The Merging Type convinces themselves that they are being abandoned because they don't care enough. This covers deeper, suppressed feelings: the person affected actually no longer has the strength or desire to constantly care about others. Repressed anger and hatred are often hidden beneath the grief.
* **The image of the other:** In order to maintain this construct, the Merging Type projects onto his counterpart that he is small, weak and dependent on him.
* **The Maximum Trigger:** The Merger Type mistakenly believes that they will be immediately abandoned as soon as they step into their own autonomy, become independent, or express that they feel anger and hatred.

## The Reality Check: The practical exercise

In order to dissolve this painful concept of attachment, the inner projections must be checked against reality. Today, the mental construct of constant threat usually only exists in one's own head.

**Exercise for the Merging Type:** Ask a close person (e.g. your life partner or therapist) the following questions directly. The important thing is to be open and willing to really hear and absorb the answer:

1. **"Are you planning to leave me?"**
2. **"Do you have a problem with me telling you that I feel anger?"**
3. **“Do you have a problem with me doing my own thing, becoming autonomous and having my own opinion?”**
4. **"Is it true that you feel small, helpless, dependent on me and don't know how to live without me?"**

> **Note on implementation:** Bravely asking these questions - which have often been unconsciously held back since childhood - can trigger a profound change in the attachment system. It allows the nervous system to learn that the old protective program is no longer needed today.
