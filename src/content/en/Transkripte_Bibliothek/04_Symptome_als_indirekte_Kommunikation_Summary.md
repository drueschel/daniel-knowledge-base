# Symptoms as indirect communication

The concepts taught are not rigid models, but rather a dynamic approach to understanding the deep, interconnected nature of symptoms, inner life and relationships.

## The method is not a static model
* **Dynamic Analysis:** Interrelationships (such as merger type and money issues) represent common patterns, not inevitable rules. A merger type does not automatically develop money problems and vice versa.
* **A Way of Working:** This approach is a method of analysis applied to individual situations, rather than a rigid conceptual construct.

## Symptoms as indirect communication
* **No isolated problems:** Symptoms and difficult external circumstances do not exist in isolation; they are deeply connected to the inner world.
* **The Language of Symptoms:** Any problem or suffering is an indirect expression of something the person is unaware of or is afraid of communicating directly.
* **Example:** Suffering from an illness to get attention can be an indirect way to show unexpressed feelings such as loneliness or sadness.
* **Remove the Fuel:** By resolving the underlying attachment conflict, the "fuel" or energy needed to produce symptoms of indirect communication disappears. This has the potential to relieve or resolve symptoms.

## The core goal: direct communication and connection
* **Shifting the focus:** The true cause of suffering is not the symptom itself, but the unconscious creation of distance from others.
* **Honest Exchange:** The practice aims to facilitate the transition from indirect communication (about symptoms) to direct, honest expression.
* **Nourishing Relationships:** By learning to stop creating distance, you open the door to complete, fulfilling and nourishing relationships.
