# What is a feeling?

In this transcript, Gopal shares his specific perspective on what feelings are and clearly distinguishes them from thoughts and physical sensations. He emphasizes that feelings are essentially always linked to a bonding context and encourages people to explore their own perceptions in a playful and non-coercive manner.

## The three levels of perception
Gopal does not differentiate between feelings and emotions, but uses a three-level model:

* **Thoughts:** The mental level consisting of concepts, sentences or images in the mind.
* **Body sensations:** The physical level, such as pain, heat, pressure, tension, fear or disgust (stress/survival instinct without an attachment context).
* **Feelings/emotions:** The heart level, which always relates to the relationship and bond with a counterpart.

## Feelings and the attachment context
According to Gopal, feelings do not arise by chance, but rather reflect the needs in a relationship:

* At their core, they revolve around relationships with other people.
* For example, grief arises when separated (the need for more closeness).
* Anger shows up when boundaries are crossed (need for more independence).

## The core feelings
Although terms such as jealousy, shame or envy are often referred to as feelings, Gopal usually sees them as a mixture of a thought construct and a physical state of distress:

* **For him, the true core feelings** at the heart level are ultimately only **sadness** and **anger** (connected with the respective poles of loneliness and hatred).
* **Joy** takes on a special role: it arises when the system is regulated (no anger, no sadness) and, according to Gopal, already passes into a more subtle, higher-level area without a pure bonding context.

## Exploring your own perception
Gopal invites you to use this perspective for your own inner research:

* **Finding your own truth:** It's completely okay to perceive things differently. It is important to increasingly trust your own perception.
* **No obligation:** Distinguishing between levels requires practice. It's about gaining clarity playfully and with joy.
* **Expanding Consciousness:** The process of self-exploration has been compared to an exciting cave discovery - it is a fulfilling path to greater awareness.
