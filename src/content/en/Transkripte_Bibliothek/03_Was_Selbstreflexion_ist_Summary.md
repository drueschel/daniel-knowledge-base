# 03 What self-reflection is

Self-reflection is understood as deep and honest self-awareness. The focus is on moving away from compulsive, mental rumination for solutions and instead grasping the current internal and external state clearly and objectively.

## Core concepts of self-perception

* **Perception instead of rumination:** It is not about desperately finding a solution to suffering through endless thinking. The crucial step is simply noticing what is happening in the present moment.
* **The Three Levels of Inner Reality:** Honestly observe what is going on within you on the three levels:
  * **Thoughts**
  * **Feelings**
  * **Body level**
* **Radical Honesty:** Only through absolute honesty with yourself can you see things more clearly and create the basis for real change.
* **The external reality (Reality Check):** It is crucial to compare the internal perception with an objective view of the external world. The brain tends to overlay current reality with old experiences and conditions.

## Focus for practice

**The reality check question:**

* "What is objective reality on the outside? For example, am I in real physical danger right now or not?"
