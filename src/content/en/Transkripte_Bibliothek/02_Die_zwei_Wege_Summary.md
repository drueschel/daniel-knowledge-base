# The two ways

During child development, we react to an emotionally inadequate or overwhelming environment - as a coping mechanism to a situation that is perceived as insoluble - in two main ways. These two basic structures of contact formation largely determine how we act in relationships as adults: either by trying to actively create a missing connection (fusion) or by giving up contact (autonomy).

## The two coping strategies (contact structures)

### 1. The merge type
* **Emergence:** The child experienced a certain level of connection and regulation, but this was inadequate, unreliable or has broken down.
* **Behavioral pattern:** A noticeable deficiency arises. The child (and later the adult) becomes very active and chases after the missing connection in order to establish, deepen or complete the connection.
* **Core characteristic:** High activity and constant effort to build or maintain relationships.

### 2. The Autonomy Type
* **Origin:** The connection to the reference persons was even more inadequate than with the Merging Type. The child realizes that no attempt at connection makes sense in this specific environment.
* **Behavior Pattern:** The child gives up trying to establish or deepen a connection.
* **Core feature:** Resignation in contact with people and the withdrawal into one's own independence.

## The interaction of both parts
* **Both parts are present:** Every person basically has both parts (fusion and autonomy) within themselves.
* **Focus in childhood:** In childhood, however, you usually had to "escape" in one of these two directions in order to stabilize the unbearable situation and survive. This led to an unhealthy focus on one strategy to the neglect of the others.
* **The goal in adulthood:** Healing means being able to allow, live out and authentically express both basic needs (connectedness and autonomy) in relationships today as an adult.

## Reflection and practice

To identify your own core strategy, ask yourself the following simplified questions:

* **Autonomy:** Have you given up contact with people internally?
* **Fusion:** Do you tend to chase people to make a connection?
