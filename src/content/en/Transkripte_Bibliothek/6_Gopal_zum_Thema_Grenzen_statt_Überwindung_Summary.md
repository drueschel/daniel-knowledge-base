# Gopal on the topic of boundaries instead of overcoming them

This recording illuminates the often misunderstood dynamic between the need for connection and the simultaneous desire for protection. Gopal explains how Developmental Trauma pushes us into an apparent dilemma between withdrawal and self-overcoming, and shows a healing third way: conscious communication of one's own limits as a means for real contact.

## The dilemma of contact and protection
* **Internalized ideas:** The fear of contact often results from the internalized idea that you have to show or say something about yourself that you don't actually want to reveal.
* **The Two Extremes of Developmental Trauma:**
  * **Withdrawal and avoidance:** You protect yourself, but as a result you often feel lonely and disconnected. A profound transformation is not possible through pure avoidance.
  * **Self-overcoming (boundary crossing):** You force yourself into contact and cross your own boundaries. However, this doesn't lead to a real connection because you're not being authentic to yourself and doing something you don't want to do.

## The solution: Communicate the border yourself
Instead of frantically trying to tear down inner walls or hiding behind them, the solution is to introduce the boundary itself into communication.

* **Protection is allowed:** As adults, we can now get in touch with our protective mechanisms and our walls. There are no longer any unsatisfied, existential child needs behind it.
* **True Contact:** Communicating one's own defenses *is* actual contact. It effectively dissolves the polarity of isolation and border crossing.
* **Adult Relationships:** In a healthy adult relationship, all boundaries and needs are allowed to exist as long as they are communicated adequately.

## Practical example of sharing
If the urge to withdraw or fear of contact arises, don't force yourself to open up. Instead, share exactly this inner state:

> *"To be honest, I'm afraid of contact and actually I don't want any contact at all right now."*
> 
> *or*
> 
> *"I'm feeling really weird today. I feel like I need a lot of protection and I don't really want to say what's going on inside me."*

**The healing effect:** Such honest and non-aggressive communication paradoxically immediately creates deep closeness. You experience that you are allowed to exist with your limitations and fears. As a rule, a noticeable feeling of joy and relief arises relatively quickly afterwards.
