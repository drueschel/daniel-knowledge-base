# Session 2: Core Skills – Essential Competencies, Diagnostics & Erotic Lexicon for Intimacy Coaches

This session delivers the foundational practical toolkit required for professional intimacy and sex coaching. Facilitated by renowned sexologist and coach educator **Onika Henry**, the lesson bridges clinical case methodology (originating with the pioneer of sex coaching, **Dr. Patti Britton**) with erotic lexicon expansion, holistic assessment using **Dennis Dailey’s Circles of Sexuality**, the transformative **SAR process** (*Sexual Attitude Reassessment*), and the **Courageous Conversations** relational communication blueprint.

---

## 1. The Bedrock of Sex Coaching: The Case of "John" (Dr. Patti Britton)

Utilizing the classic case study of "John"—a high-functioning mid-career man presenting with sudden erectile dysfunction, shame, and marital withdrawal—Onika Henry illustrates the operational contrast between traditional clinical therapy and somatic sex coaching:
- **The Pitfall of Symptom Eradication:** Conventional medicine prescribes PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra); traditional talk therapy spends years dissecting childhood dynamics. Both often fail because the client's neuromuscular system remains trapped in performance panic.
- **The Somatic Coaching Roadmap:**
  1. *De-escalation:* Sexual pressure is neutralized immediately by instituting an absolute moratorium on intercourse and orgasmic goals (*Sensate Focus*).
  2. *Autonomic Retuning:* John is trained to shift breathing from shallow apical panic into deep diaphragmatic and pelvic resonance.
  3. *Full-Body Sensuality:* The penis is dethroned from its high-stakes pedestal; the rest of the body is awakened as a responsive erogenous field.

## 3. Dennis Dailey’s 5 Circles of Sexuality

1. **Sensuality:** Experiencing the world through the sensory apparatus; skin contact, body image, physiological arousal, pleasure divorced from performance.
2. **Intimacy:** Emotional vulnerability, emotional bonding, sharing secret truths, mutual risk-taking, and secure attachment.
3. **Sexual Identity:** Biological sex, gender identity, gender presentation, sexual orientation. The core felt sense of self.
4. **Sexual Health & Reproduction:** Anatomy, reproductive biology, contraception, STI management, fertility, pregnancy, and hormonal shifts across life stages.
5. **Sexualization:** Using sexuality as an instrument of control, influence, manipulation, or coercion (ranging from playful seduction and flirting to harassment, exploitation, and abuse).

## 4. The SAR Principle: Sexual Attitude Reassessment

The **SAR** is a mandatory, intensive self-interrogation process required of ethical sexological professionals:
- **Core Purpose:** Surfacing and desensitizing personal triggers, cultural taboos, religious programming, and visceral disgust reactions.
- **Key Arenas:** BDSM/kink, consensual non-monogamy (CNM), sex work, extreme fetishes, gender affirmation procedures, sexuality in aging and disability.
- **Practitioner Axiom:** A coach cannot safely guide a client further than the boundaries of their own unconditioned presence. If a coach internally winces at a client’s kink, the client's nervous system detects the rupture instantly.

## 5. Relational Communication: The Courageous Conversations Framework

Developed by Onika Henry to guide couples through high-vulnerability intimate impasses, this 5-stage protocol ensures emotional safety:

```
[ STAGE 1: Anchor the Container ] ──► Agree on sacred time and space; zero screens
                │
                ▼
[ STAGE 2: Own the Vulnerability ]──► Name internal nervousness ("I feel vulnerable saying this...")
                │
                ▼
[ STAGE 3: Observation & Longing ]──► Objective observation + personal desire without blame
                │
                ▼
[ STAGE 4: Nervous System Check ] ──► "How is this landing in your body? What are you feeling?"
                │
                ▼
[ STAGE 5: Co-create Micro-Steps] ──► Agree on small, manageable, low-stakes experiments
```

### Exemplary Coaching Dialogue:
- *Partner A:* "I love you, and I feel butterflies in my throat admitting this. I miss our tender touch, and I yearn for us to spend 15 minutes cuddling skin-to-skin without any expectation of sex. How does that sound to your body?"
- *Partner B (coached):* Pauses, takes a long exhalation, grounds into their sit bones, and responds from somatic calm rather than defensive panic.

## 6. Common Client Patterns & Somatic Interventions

| Client Presentation | Somatic Indicator | Coach Intervention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intellectual Dissociation** | Rapid monologue, eyes scanning the ceiling, tense posture | Somatic interruption: "Pause. Take a long exhale. Where does that narrative register in your stomach right now?" |
| **Linguistic Avoidance** | Nervous laughter, stammering, using childish euphemisms | Vocal normalization exercise: Coach invites the client to slowly and clearly repeat the anatomical term three times while feeling their feet on the floor. |
| **Intimacy Discrepancy Conflict** | Finger-pointing, defensive crossed arms | Map the friction via the *Circles of Sexuality*: Is the deficit located in *Sensuality*, or is the relationship suffering from an emotional drought in *Intimacy*? |
