# Session 1: Holding the Frame – The Coach-Client Relationship & Ethical Boundaries

This opening session of Module 2 establishes the professional container and ethical architecture essential for somatic intimacy coaching. Facilitated by **Kai Cheng Thom**, coaches master the legal boundaries defining their non-clinical **Scope of Practice**, the practice of personal grounding via the **Pelvic Center of Gravity**, the comprehensive mechanics of **Dr. Betty Martin’s Wheel of Consent**, and protocols for navigating erotic transference and countertransference.

---

## 1. Scope of Practice: The 4 Modalities of Somatic Work

Maintaining legal protection and uncompromising ethical integrity requires intimacy coaches to clearly differentiate their practice from adjacent therapeutic modalities:

| Modality | Primary Focus & Objective | Touch Policy | Legal & Regulatory Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Life / Relationship Coaching** | Cognitive, goal-oriented, future-focused; communication tools | Zero touch; purely conversation-based | Non-clinical; unregulated coaching profession |
| **Somatic Intimacy Coaching** *(this program)* | Somatic inquiry, de-shaming, autonomic co-regulation, touch rituals | **Zero coach-to-client touch.** Client self-touch or partner-to-partner practices only | Non-clinical; no medical diagnoses; experiential education |
| **Somatic Psychotherapy (e.g., SE, NARM)** | Resolving clinical trauma, PTSD, developmental arrest, personality disorders | Minimal grounding touch (shoulder, back) if clinically indicated | Licensed healthcare profession (psychologists, therapists) |
| **Hands-On Somatic Sexology / Bodywork** | Genital mapping, tissue scar release, pelvic de-armoring | Direct hands-on touch with clothed or unclothed clients | Highly regulated; requires specific licensure; high liability |

> [!CAUTION]
> **The Inviolable Rule of Somatic Intimacy Coaching:**  
> Within this coaching paradigm, **physical or erotic contact between coach and client is strictly prohibited.** Coaches facilitate clients in exploring self-touch (*Self-Touch*) or guide couples in structuring partner touch practices. This boundary protects vulnerable clients from exploitation and insulates practitioners from malpractice allegations.

## 2. Somatic Anchoring: The Coach's Pelvic Center of Gravity

When clients disclose traumatic memories, visceral shame, or intense erotic longings, an unregulated coach risks either freezing into detached intellectualization or drowning in empathetic distress (*Empathic Distress*).

### Protocol for Pelvic Anchoring:
1. **Dropping the Center of Gravity:** The coach continuously redirects attention downward from the prefrontal cortex into the pelvic floor and ischial tuberosities (sit bones).
2. **Gravity as an Autonomic Anchor:** Feeling the physical contact of the pelvis against the seat preserves grounded presence, alertness, and stability.
3. **Resonance without Fusion:** An anchored pelvis allows the practitioner to hold a resonant field for intense emotions and erotic charge without taking the client’s dysregulation into their own tissues.

## 3. Dr. Betty Martin’s Wheel of Consent

Developed by **Dr. Betty Martin**, the *Wheel of Consent* is one of the most transformative frameworks in relational and sexual somatic education. It disentangles physical touch across two distinct, perpendicular axes:
1. **Who is physically executing the action?** (*Doer* vs. *Receiver*)
2. **Who is the action for? / Whose pleasure or purpose is being served?** (*For Whose Benefit?*)

### The 4 Quadrants Explored:
1. **Serving (Giving a Gift):** The Doer dedicates their time and hands solely to the other person's pleasure. *Shadow:* Resentment, boundary erosion, martyred burnout.
2. **Accepting (Receiving a Gift):** The Receiver relaxes and soaks in a gift they explicitly requested. *Shadow:* Guilt, feeling undeserving, anxiety about being a burden.
3. **Taking (Exploring with Permission):** The Doer satisfies their own sensory curiosity on the partner's body (with clear consent). *Shadow:* Entitlement, boundary violations, assault.
4. **Allowing (Granting Access):** The Receiver opens their body to the partner's curiosity because they genuinely delight in giving that gift. *Shadow:* Fawning, passive enduring (*Enduring*), silent compliance.

## 4. Experiential Integration: The Two-Hand Touch Protocol

To move the Wheel of Consent from theoretical comprehension into cellular memory, coaches utilize the *Two-Hand Touch* practice:
- **Movement A (Taking):** The client or coach caresses the fabric of their own clothing or thigh with the conscious focus: *"How does this texture feel against MY fingertips?"* (Tracking self-centered sensory enjoyment).
- **Movement B (Serving):** The hand touches the same spot with the conscious focus: *"What does my leg need right now? Does it crave warmth, firm pressure, or stillness?"* (Tracking other-centered nourishment).
- **The Somatic Insight:** The outer mechanical action is identical—the internal somatic intention completely reorganizes the autonomic response of both parties!
